Beliefs 
Behavior: Social distancing
Behavioral beliefs - beliefs about the outcome 
Positive: “Social distancing will reduce spreading of the virus” Negative: “I’m not in a risk group, thus, I will not get sick” Normative beliefs - beliefs about what others might think Positive: “I believe that my parents want me to do this” Negative: “Everyone around me think it is okay to still meet up” Efficacy beliefs - beliefs about whether people think they can do it Positive: “I can stay inside most of the time anyway” Negative: “I don’t know how long 6 feet is”

Implications for advertising 
1. Determine WHAT behavior you want to influence 
2. Determine target group (WHO) 
3. Research whether it is attitudes, subjective norms or perceived  behavioral control that matters in purchasing (if purchasing is the goal) 
4. Identify the specific beliefs that influence the purchasing decision
Think like an advertiser! 
● Select a product 
● Describe your target audience 
● List what you think may be attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived  behavioral control that would influence the behavioral intentions to  purchase the item 
● Create a campaign idea to address one of these factors that you think  may be important
