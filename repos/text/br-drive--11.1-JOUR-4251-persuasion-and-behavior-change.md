Goals, habits, and behaviors
Habits
Learned sequences of acts that have become automatic responses to 
speciﬁc cues and are functional in obtaining certain goals or end states
Something becomes a habit if performed frequently under stable conditions
Example: Brushing your teeth
Goal dependent automaticity: you have to have the intention to do it, 
but then once you start, you just know how
