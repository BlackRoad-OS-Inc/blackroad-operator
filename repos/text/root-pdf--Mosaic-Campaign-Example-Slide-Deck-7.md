## Page 1

IDEA BRIEF
Client: Mercury Marine 
Project: Campaign Strategy
Team: Hannah Kil, Ellissa Cleveland, Alexa Heller, Trevor Thompson, 
Sydney Johnson, Udita Goel
Brand personality: Fresh. Reliable. Exciting.
Brand tone: Inspiring, confident, sentimental.
What problem are we solving?
The main problem we are trying to solve is the lack of female engagement and participation in boating. Throughout 
our research, we determined the core conflict between females and boating to be  a lack of awareness, confidence, and 
motivation to be a part of the boating community. We want women to understand that boating is no longer a huge 
undertaking, and encourage them to leave behind their passive tendencies that are making them reluctant to be a part 
of a new lifestyle. 
The one compelling message:
Navigate new waters, together.
Who are we talking to & what do we know about them?
We’re talking to 45-54 year old women with families and a high 
discretionary income, AKA “family-focused relaxers”. For this woman, it’s 
family first. She takes time out of her own day to make sure everyone else’s 
is perfect. Whenever she tries taking time for herself, she ends up occupied 
with something or the other. But, she loves being a wife and mom and 
accepts the lack of control as part of the lifestyle. She tries to make any time 
spent outside of work and school extra meaningful, and has found that 
being outdoors together offers the perfect combination of bonding, 
educating, and relaxing.

Why should they care?
She should care because she deserves to be included in the narrative. She 
should be able to take control while keeping what’s most important at the 
center of it - her family and loved ones. To adopt a new label for herself and 
add a new feather in her cap. To take the hand that will pull her into the 
middle of the dance floor and will allow her to live, laugh, and love, boldly. 
Who/what is the enemy?
The enemy is other outdoor recreational activities/brands. ATV brands, 
hiking brands, etc: other activities that would take time and could prevent 
someone from spending time participating in boating. 
The enemy is also negative attitudes. We need to push against our audiences' 
immediate assumptions, and of those around them, that hold them back 
from wholeheartedly participating in something they would love.
Our final enemy is the age of the short-term. In order to get the wave of 
participation we truly desire for Mercury, our audience can't just dip their 
toes in the water. We need to show them that this is a movement, not just a 
campaign.
What does success look like?
Our main goal is to get that conversation started and to get our audience 
angeged.

women users. 
•
Increase female user traffic on Mercury Marine website by 15%
•
Increase female engagement on social media by 15%
