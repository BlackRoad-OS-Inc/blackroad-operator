Understanding each stage of the process will help you feel confident and informed throughout the entire experience.

1. Buyer Representation Paperwork:

Before proceeding, we must complete the buyer representation paperwork to establish our working relationship. This agreement covers three crucial points:

a) Agency Disclosure: It is essential to clarify that I am acting as your real estate agent and will represent your best interests throughout the buying process. As your buyer's agent, I have a legal obligation to prioritize your needs and negotiate on your behalf.

b) Buyer Representation Agreement: This document solidifies our partnership and outlines the terms and duration of our working relationship. It ensures that you have a dedicated agent to assist you in finding your ideal home.

c) Affiliated eXp Business Disclosure: eXp Realty has affiliated businesses that offer various services. I will explain what this means and how it may benefit you during the buying process.

2. Preapproval Process:

Before we begin searching for your dream home, obtaining preapproval is a crucial step. Here's what you need to know:

a) Preapproval vs. Prequalification: Prequalification provides an estimate of the loan amount you may qualify for based on the information you provide to the lender. Preapproval, on the other hand, involves a more in-depth assessment of your financial situation and creditworthiness. It strengthens your position as a buyer and gives you a better idea of your budget.

b) Preapproval Qualifications: To get preapproved, you'll need to provide financial documents, such as income verification, bank statements, and credit reports. The lender will evaluate this information to determine the mortgage amount you qualify for.

c) VA Loans: If you are eligible for a VA loan, it can be an excellent option for veterans and active-duty service members, offering favorable terms and benefits.

3. Looking for a Home:

Once you are preapproved, we can begin the exciting search for your new home. During this phase:

a) Booking Showings: Let me know which properties catch your interest, and I'll schedule showings for us to explore together in person.

b) Making Offers: As soon as we find the right property, we can write an offer. We'll consider the condition of the home, potential major fixes, and comparable market analyses to determine a competitive offer price.

c) Conditions and Negotiations: We can include conditions in the offer to protect your interests, such as requesting repairs based on the inspection report. Negotiations with the seller may follow until we reach mutually agreeable terms.

4. The Offer and Its Contents:

Here's what to expect in the offer:

- Closing Date: The date when the transfer of ownership takes place.

- Earnest Money: A deposit, typically 1-3% of the purchase price, demonstrating your commitment to the purchase.

- Down Payment: The initial payment you make towards the home's total cost.

- Inspection Period: The timeframe to conduct a professional inspection of the property.

- Appraisal Guarantees: Ensuring that the property's appraised value aligns with the purchase price.

- Seller Paid Closing Costs: Any costs the seller agrees to cover as part of the deal.

- Buyer Paid Closing Costs: Costs you are responsible for paying during the closing process.

- Financing Deadlines: Specific timelines related to securing the loan.

- Type of Loan: The type of mortgage you are applying for, such as conventional, FHA, or VA.

5. Closing Process:

Upon acceptance of the offer:

- You'll need to submit earnest money within two days of the accepted offer date.

- Within 3-10 days, a professional inspection will occur, and you will be responsible for the inspection cost.

- Based on the inspection report, we'll decide on any repairs and who will cover the expenses.

- The seller will have until the final walkthrough to make any agreed-upon repairs; otherwise, the contract may be voided.

- Prior to closing, ensure all necessary preparations are made, such as ordering movers and switching utility services.

- The last step before closing day is the final walkthrough. A day or two before closing, we will visit the property again and ensure that it is in the condition everyone agreed to.

At closing, bring the required identification forms and sign the paperwork. After this exciting event, you'll receive the keys to your new home, and it will officially be yours!
