## Slide 8

Change of Metrics for Nielsen

In 2022, Nielsen will shift away from ratings-based measurements for advertising

They will now instead measure viewership through engagements

Nielsen will now also be measuring “broadband-only homes”, meaning consumers that receive all of their entertainment through the internet/streaming

They will now have the ability to track the viewership or media consumption across multiple different devices and platforms

## Slide 13

Target Audience Definition and Why It Matters

Definition: the specific group of consumers most likely to want your product or service, and therefore, the group of people who should see your ad campaigns. Target audience may be dictated by age, gender, income, location, interests or a myriad of other factors.

Why it matters: it allows you to focus your advertising dollars and brand message on a specific market that is more likely to buy from you than potential audiences. This is a much more affordable, efficient, and effective way to reach potential clients and generate business.


## Slide 14

Where to begin building your target audience:

Look at your current customer base and identify their common characteristics and interest:

Why do you think they use your products or like your brand?

Which of your current customers drive the most revenue?

Find your niche:

Look at the audiences your competitors are targeting and who their current customers are.

Analyze your products and services:

Match up the products you offer with the needs of consumer. Develop a list of the  benefits of your products and make a list of the types of people who have a need that your benefit fulfills. This will help you identify your value proposition.

Value prop definition: a statement that answers the 'why' someone should do business with you. It should convince a potential customer why your service or product will be of more value to them than similar offerings from your competition.


## Slide 15

Demographic and Psychographic Building – Determine who is likely to buy

Demographics: Age, location, gender, income level, education level, marital or family status, occupation, ethnicity, etc.

Psychographics: Personality, attitudes, values, interests/hobbies, lifestyles, behaviors.

Use psychographics to determine the types of channels your target uses and understand how your brand/product fits into their life. Understand what features are most attractive.


## Slide 16

Is your target audience viable?

Once you've decided on a target market, be sure to consider these questions:

Are there enough people who fit my criteria?

Will my target really benefit from my product/service? Will they see a need for it?

Do I understand what drives my target to make decisions?

Can they afford my product/service?

Can I reach them with my message? Are they easily accessible?


## Slide 17

Considerations

You can have several audiences and tailor your messaging accordingly.

That said, if the same message works for two target audiences, they can likely be combined.

Your audiences need to be big enough to be able to reach them.


## Slide 18

Where to find this information:

Look for survey results, or consider conducting a survey of your own.

Ask your current customers for feedback.
