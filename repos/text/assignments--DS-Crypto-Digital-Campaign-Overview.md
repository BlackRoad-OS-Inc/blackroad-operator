## Page 5

Target Audience
●
Older gen z 
○
Mark is just finishing up class on a Friday at his 4 year public university, before going 
to work at his local Target, Mark is hoping to get a quick bite to eat somewhere close 
by to where he works. Usually, Mark prefers Taco Bell or McDonald’s, but once a 
week, he will treat himself to Chipotle, a fast food alternative that costs a few dollars 
more per meal. The bottom line for Mark is that he will eat wherever he gets the most 
amount of food for the least amount of money. He cares significantly about saving 
the money he earns, and chooses food options that are almost always a safe bet. 
After class this Friday, Mark may be open to trying somewhere different, if it offers 
the right incentive with the same “safe fast food chain bet” nature.
○
The target audience of crypto is gen z and most college students are looking for the 
same things Mark is.
