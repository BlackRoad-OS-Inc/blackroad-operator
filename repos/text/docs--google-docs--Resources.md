Subject: Licensing Steps for Selling Individual Annuities

Hi [Advisor Name],

Below is an outline of the steps you need to complete in order to become licensed to sell individual annuities through Securian:

1. **Verify Licensing and Appointments**

• Confirm that you hold the appropriate state licenses and that you’re appointed to sell Securian annuity products.

• If any updates are needed, contact the Central Licensing Unit at 1-866-889-6107 or via centrallicensing@securian.com.

2. **Complete Product-Specific Training**

• Access the online training modules available through Quest CE at [https://learn.questce.com/Securian/](https://learn.questce.com/Securian/).

• Ensure you complete the training by the state’s effective date for the products you wish to sell.

3. **Finish the Required Continuing Education Course**

• Complete the one-time, four-hour CE training course as mandated under the NAIC Suitability in Annuity Transactions Model Regulation.

• Licensed professionals prior to the regulation’s effective date typically have six months to finish this requirement, while new licenses must complete the CE course before selling any annuities.

4. **Notify Securian of Your Training Completion**

• Once your training is complete, fax proof of your CE course completion to Securian’s Licensing team at 1-651-665-7256 or call them at 1-866-889-6107 for further instructions.

5. **Maintain Ongoing Compliance**

• Stay current with your licensing and complete any additional training updates as required if there are product changes or state-specific modifications.

Please let me know if you have any questions or need further clarification on any of these steps.

Subject: Getting Started Selling Securian Individual Annuities

Hi [Name],

Thank you for your interest in selling Securian Individual Annuities. Here’s a quick overview of the steps to get you started:

• **Verify Your Licensing & Appointments**

– Ensure your state licenses are current and that you’re appointed to sell Securian annuities.

• **Complete Product-Specific Training**

– Access our online training modules (available through Quest CE) and complete the required product-specific training by your state’s effective date.

• **Finish Your Continuing Education Course**

– Complete the one-time, four-hour CE course. If you were licensed before the regulation’s effective date, you typically have up to six months to finish. New licensees must complete this course before selling any annuities.

• **Notify Us of Your Training Completion**

– Once you’ve finished your training, please fax proof of completion to our Licensing team or call them to ensure there are no interruptions in processing new business.

For more detailed information, please visit our “Get Started Selling” page:
