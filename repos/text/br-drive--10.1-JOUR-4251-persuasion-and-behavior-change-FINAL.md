## Page 4

Beliefs
Behavioral beliefs - beliefs about the outcome
Positive: “Social distancing will reduce spreading of the virus”
Negative: “I’m not in a risk group, thus, I will not get sick”
Normative beliefs - beliefs about what others might think
Positive: “I believe that my parents want me to do this”
Negative: “Everyone around me think it is okay to still meet up”
Efﬁcacy beliefs - beliefs about whether people think they can do it
Positive: “I can stay inside most of the time anyway”
Negative: “I don’t know how long 6 feet is”
Behavior: Social distancing

Implications for advertising
1.
Determine WHAT behavior you want to inﬂuence
2.
Determine target group (WHO)
3.
Research whether it is attitudes, subjective norms or perceived 
behavioral control that matters in purchasing (if purchasing is the goal)

Identify the speciﬁc beliefs that inﬂuence the purchasing decision
