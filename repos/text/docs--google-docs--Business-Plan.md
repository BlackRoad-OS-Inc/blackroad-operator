To effectively **monitor, track, and progress** your **Texas territory wholesaling business plan** using **Excel**, you can create a structured workbook that reflects the key stages of your pipeline, the KPIs for each stage, and the tasks and processes associated with them. Here’s an outline of how to set up your **Excel workbook** for optimal tracking:

### **1. Workbook Structure Overview:**

You will have **multiple sheets** within the workbook, each focusing on a different aspect of your pipeline. These sheets will be interconnected so that data flows smoothly across them for analysis and reporting.

#### **Sheet Breakdown:**

1. **Dashboard** – High-level KPI tracking & quick visual indicators.

2. **Lead Tracker** – Comprehensive tracking of leads, including status, engagement, and conversion.

3. **Activities Tracker** – Monitoring daily, weekly, and monthly tasks associated with sales processes.

4. **KPI Tracking** – Track progress toward your SMART goals and sales KPIs.

5. **Data Accuracy Tracker** – To verify and monitor contact data.

6. **Sales Results** – Track actual sales, deal size, closed/won ratios, etc.

---

### **2. Dashboard Sheet:**

This will be a **high-level summary** that allows you to quickly see the performance of key areas.

#### Columns:

1. **KPI Name** (e.g., Lead Conversion Rate, Sales Cycle Length, Win Rate, etc.)

2. **Target for 2025** (SMART goal for the year)

3. **Current Week/Month Actual**

4. **Cumulative Year-to-Date** (Year-to-Date performance)

5. **Variance** (Difference between target and actual)

6. **Status Indicator** (Green, Yellow, Red – color-coded for progress)

#### Example:

| **KPI**                  | **2025 Target** | **Current Week** | **Year-to-Date** | **Variance** | **Status** |

|--------------------------|-----------------|------------------|------------------|--------------|------------|

| **Lead Conversion Rate** | 30%             | 28%              | 29%              | -1%          | Yellow     |

| **Sales Cycle Length**   | 30 Days         | 32 Days          | 31 Days          | +2 Days      | Red        |

| **Win Rate**             | 50%             | 45%              | 47%              | -3%          | Yellow     |

---

### **3. Lead Tracker Sheet:**

This sheet will help track individual leads and their progress through the pipeline.

#### Columns:

1. **Lead ID** – Unique identifier for each lead.

2. **Advisor Name**

3. **Firm/Broker Dealer** – E.g., Cetera, LPL, etc.

4. **Lead Source** – E.g., Inbound, Outbound, Referral, Event.

5. **Initial Contact Date** – The date when the first outreach was made.

6. **Last Contact Date** – The date of the most recent interaction.

7. **Lead Status** – E.g., "Prospecting," "Qualified," "Follow-up," "Closed/Won," "Closed/Lost."

8. **Annuity Usage** – Does the advisor use annuities? Yes/No.

9. **Next Steps/Actions** – Specific tasks or follow-ups.

10. **Appointment Set?** – Yes/No.

11. **Conversion Rate** – How far they are in the process (e.g., percentage through each stage).

#### Example:

| **Lead ID** | **Advisor Name** | **Firm**    | **Lead Source** | **Initial Contact Date** | **Last Contact Date** | **Lead Status** | **Annuity Usage** | **Next Steps**              | **Appointment Set?** | **Conversion Rate** |

|-------------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|--------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------------------|----------------------|---------------------|

| 001         | John Doe         | Cetera      | Outbound        | 2025-01-10               | 2025-01-15            | Qualified       | Yes               | Send proposal, follow up call | Yes                  | 75%                 |

| 002         | Jane Smith       | LPL         | Referral        | 2025-01-12               | 2025-01-14            | Prospecting     | No                | Email resources, call next week | No                   | 20%                 |

**Notes**:

- **Lead Status** will have a dropdown list (Prospecting, Qualified, Closed/Won, etc.) for consistency.

- **Next Steps** column will indicate the next actionable item for each lead (e.g., send proposal, schedule meeting).

- **Conversion Rate** can be calculated by dividing the current stage number (e.g., 3 for "Qualified") by the total stages (5 total stages).

---

### **4. Activities Tracker Sheet:**

This sheet will track the **daily, weekly, and monthly tasks** for each advisor.

#### Columns:

1. **Task ID** – Unique ID for each task.

2. **Advisor Name**

3. **Task Type** – E.g., Initial Call, Follow-up, Appointment Set, Data Accuracy Verification.

4. **Due Date** – When the task is due.

5. **Completion Date** – Date when the task was completed.

6. **Status** – E.g., Pending, In Progress, Completed.

7. **Task Notes** – Brief notes regarding the interaction or outcome of the task.

#### Example:

| **Task ID** | **Advisor Name** | **Task Type**         | **Due Date** | **Completion Date** | **Status** | **Task Notes**           |

|-------------|------------------|-----------------------|--------------|---------------------|------------|--------------------------|

| T001        | John Doe         | Initial Call          | 2025-01-10   | 2025-01-10          | Completed  | Left voicemail, sent email |

| T002        | Jane Smith       | Data Accuracy Check   | 2025-01-12   | 2025-01-13          | Completed  | Verified assistant’s number |

| T003        | Mike Johnson     | Follow-up Call        | 2025-01-13   |                     | Pending    |                          |

**Notes**:

- For **Task Type**, you can use a **dropdown list** to select from predefined tasks (e.g., initial call, follow-up, appointment).

- **Status** will indicate where the task stands (Pending, In Progress, Completed).

### **5. KPI Tracking Sheet:**

This sheet will help you track your **KPIs** across different stages of the pipeline (Prospecting, Data Gathering, Pre-Sales, Sales, Post-Sales).

#### Columns:

1. **KPI Name** – E.g., Lead Conversion Rate, Sales Cycle Length, Win Rate.

2. **Target** – The goal you want to achieve for each KPI.

3. **Week 1** – Results for Week 1.

4. **Week 2** – Results for Week 2.

### **1. Dashboard Sheet (High-Level Overview)**

#### **Purpose:**

The **Dashboard** will provide a snapshot of your overall performance, focusing on **KPIs**, progress toward goals, and pipeline health.

#### **Key Columns:**

1. **KPI Name** – List of all the key performance indicators you will be tracking (e.g., Lead Conversion Rate, Sales Cycle Length, Win Rate).

2. **2025 Target** – The numerical target for each KPI based on your SMART goals for the year.

3. **Current Week/Month Actual** – Actual performance for the current week or month.

4. **Year-to-Date (YTD)** – Cumulative performance year-to-date.

5. **Variance** – The difference between the target and actual values. This helps identify areas that need attention.

6. **Status Indicator** – Color-coded cell (Green, Yellow, Red) to visually indicate if the KPI is on track (Green), needs attention (Yellow), or is off track (Red).

#### **Example:**

| **KPI Name**              | **2025 Target** | **Current Week** | **Month 1 Actual** | **Year-to-Date (YTD)** | **Variance** | **Status** |

|---------------------------|-----------------|------------------|--------------------|------------------------|--------------|------------|

| **Lead Conversion Rate**   | 30%             | 28%              | 29%                | 29%                    | -1%          | Yellow     |

| **Sales Cycle Length**     | 30 Days         | 32 Days          | 31 Days            | 31.5 Days              | +2 Days      | Red        |

| **Win Rate**               | 50%             | 45%              | 47%                | 46%                    | -3%          | Yellow     |

| **Client Retention Rate**  | 85%             | 88%              | 87%                | 87.5%                  | +2%          | Green      |

| **Lead-to-Client Ratio**   | 10:1            | 11:1             | 10.5:1             | 10:1                   | +1           | Green      |

**How to Set Up:**

- In **Column A**, list all the KPIs you’re tracking.

- In **Columns B-D**, manually input or link the actual data you’ll be collecting weekly or monthly (e.g., using data from your **Lead Tracker** and **Sales Results** sheets).

- In **Column E**, use basic formulas (e.g., `=B2-C2`) to calculate variances between targets and actual results.

- Use **conditional formatting** to highlight KPIs in Green, Yellow, or Red based on the variance.

### **Daily Schedule for Annuity Wholesaler - Texas Territory**

---

#### **8:00 AM - 8:30 AM: Morning Review & Prioritization**

- **Review Dashboard:** Check key KPIs (Lead Conversion Rate, Sales Cycle, Win Rate) and adjust your focus for the day.

- **Review Calendar:** Confirm any appointments or meetings for the day.

- **Prioritize Tasks:** Use your **Activities Tracker** and **Lead Tracker** to prioritize follow-ups, cold calls, and data accuracy calls.

---

#### **8:30 AM - 9:30 AM: Follow-up Calls (Service & Task Management)**

- **Service Status Tasks:** Focus on any leads or accounts needing attention (e.g., service-related issues or unresolved tasks).

- **Follow-up on Missed Calls:** Call back anyone who left a voicemail or missed your call the previous day.

- **Address Email Inquiries:** Call advisors who sent emails the prior day that need follow-up.

---

#### **9:30 AM - 11:00 AM: Lead Follow-ups & Engagement Calls**

- **Top Advisors:** Call advisors who sold Securian annuities in the last 365 days. Focus on high-value producers (based on production intelligence score).

- **Data Accuracy Calls:** Make **5 data accuracy calls** per day to ensure the contact information for advisors is correct (phone numbers, assistants, etc.).

- **Engagement Calls:** For advisors who have recently opened your emails or interacted with your content, make a quick call to continue engagement and move them forward in the pipeline.

---

#### **11:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Data Discovery & Prospecting**

- **Pre-Sales Data Discovery:** Call new advisors or those who haven't been contacted in a while for initial data gathering. Ask about their use of annuities, preferred carriers, and gather information on their client base.

- **Targeted Prospecting:** Reach out to potential high-probability leads based on their firm (e.g., Cetera, LPL, Osaic) or past engagement.

---

#### **12:00 PM - 1:00 PM: Lunch Break**

---

#### **1:00 PM - 2:00 PM: Sales Follow-ups & Illustrations**

- **Follow-up on Open Illustrations:** Call advisors who have received annuity illustrations but have not yet followed up. Ask if they have any questions and offer assistance.

- **Review Pipeline Health:** Check **Lead Tracker** to see if any leads need to be moved through stages (Prospecting to Data Gathering, Data Gathering to Pre-Sales).

---

#### **2:00 PM - 3:30 PM: Advisor Onboarding & Training**

- **New Appointments & Appointments Set the Day Before:** Call any new advisors appointed the previous day to onboard them or follow up from a previous interaction.

- **Training and Resources:** Send training materials, service forms, and any relevant resources to new or underperforming advisors. Ensure they have the support needed to engage with annuities.

---

#### **3:30 PM - 4:30 PM: CRM Updates & Data Entry**

- **Update Lead Tracker:** Add any new contacts, update statuses for follow-up tasks, and ensure accurate data entry for all leads and advisor interactions.

- **Sales Results:** Enter any new closed deals or sales from the day, including deal size, commission, and follow-up actions.

---

#### **4:30 PM - 5:00 PM: End-of-Day Review & Prep for Tomorrow**

- **Review Completed Tasks:** Ensure all daily tasks (calls, emails, follow-ups) have been completed. Update the **Activities Tracker** with notes on outcomes.

- **Prepare for Tomorrow:** Set your calendar and task priorities for the next day based on remaining high-priority items and follow-ups.

- **Wrap Up with KPI Check:** Review KPIs to check if there are any red flags or areas requiring more focus tomorrow.

---

### **Key Reminders for the Day:**

- **KPIs & SMART Goals:** Stay aligned with your KPIs (Lead Conversion Rate, Sales Cycle, Win Rate) to ensure daily actions align with your larger goals.

- **Pipeline Velocity:** Ensure you’re consistently moving leads through the stages: **Prospecting** → **Data Gathering** → **Pre-Sales** → **Sales** → **Post-Sales**.

- **Client Retention:** Don’t forget post-sales follow-up for existing clients to prevent churn and increase upsell opportunities.
