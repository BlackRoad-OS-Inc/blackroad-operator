## Slide 2

IKEA Catalog Moves to Pinterest + “Renocations”

Katie Sandler


## Slide 4

Why this transition?

Pinterest is full of DIY-ers and how-to-ers, which are IKEA’s target audience

IKEA noticed its promotions weren’t doing well, felt stale

Created a built-in product questionnaire (4% completion rate)

This led to 25,000 IKEA Pinterest boards

The response was measured through engagement and conversion to the product pages


## Slide 5

What is the goal of the transition?

To take advantage of the shoppability of Pinterest users

To create a more concrete connection between Pinterest and IKEA

To reach IKEA’s target audience on a platform they tend to use

Eventually, to debut the interactive tool Renocation


## Slide 6

Renocations

Renocations

An interactive tool that gives the consumer customized shoppable pins

after a short questionnaire


## Slide 7

Target audience

Women, aged early 20s to late 30s, maybe even into 40s

DIY-ers and how-to-ers

Those with quite a bit of spending money

Those who have an eye for decorating, but keep a budget in mind

Those who are already familiar with Pinterest and IKEA


## Slide 8

Effectiveness

Quite effective

The quiz was easy, quick, fun

Low commitment tool to use

Perfect for when you’re “scrolling” or “browsing”

Shoppable links take you straight to the IKEA website (easy conversion)

COVID-19

People have been spending more time at home than usual

Renovating became a huge trend


## Slide 9

Improvement

Consider creating a similar tool for Facebook Marketplace and the Instagram shopping feature

Advertise the tool in IKEA stores

Make the quiz a little longer and more specific

The existing questions felt pretty generic

People love answering questions about themselves

## Slide 11

Pinterest Lens

Emily Schultz


## Slide 12

Started in 2017 as a way to see pinterest posts of similar items that you put in lens

2020 - added shop tab directly to Pinterest Lense results

https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/pinterest-lens


## Slide 13

WHY

Goal - increase user satisfaction and revenue by making pinterest “shoppable”

Monetizing pins with “buy it” button

Promoted pins based on user data

Target Audience - Women 18-35

Why is this effective? - Capitalizes on shoppable social media

Accessable, easy, and “lazy”


## Slide 14

Strengths

Capitalizes on popularity of online shopping and shopping through the app

Easily accessible

Customized experience

Every product pin links directly to the checkout page on the retailers website

Catches users at “moment of discovery”

Partnered with Target and Samsung

Google also created lens technology for products like clothing and food to capitalize off of pinterest (https://lens.google/)


## Slide 15

Weaknesses

Search results don’t go beyond pinterest platform

Store needs to have product attached to pinterest to be seen

Easily copied

Does not set itself apart from other shoppable apps

Only people who already use pinterest will use it

Niche audience

Improvements?

Why is it easier than typing in keywords?

Promote the lens feature outside of pinterest to bring in new users.
