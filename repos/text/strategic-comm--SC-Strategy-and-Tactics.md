## Slide 16

STP Approach

SEGMENTING: Breaking down diverse markets into manageable segments.

TARGETING: Choosing specific segments as the focal point for marketing efforts.

POSITIONING: Aligning the marketing mix to yield distinctive appeal for the target segment.


## Slide 17

STP Approach - Folgers

SEGMENTING: People prepare coffee at home for different reasons and drink coffee for different reasons. Not all people want a leisurely cup of coffee while reading the paper. Some want to wake up and realize morning is tough.

TARGETING: Young adults who make coffee at home to get going. Not morning people.

POSITIONING: Funny. We get it – mornings are rough. We can help you survive those people trying to get you going in the morning.

Budgeting
