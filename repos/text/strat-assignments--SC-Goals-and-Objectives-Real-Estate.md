KATZ – 3201 worksheet
GOALS and OBJECTIVES
Goals are defined in this class as abstract items you wish to accomplish (ie. increase sales, 
increase volunteers for the organization, increase customer engagement)
Objectives are defined in this class as specific and measurable items that you wish to 
accomplish – they should have a measurable component and be date-bound (ie. increase sales 
by 20% among targeted consumers by December 31, 2024; increase the number of twitter 
followers by 10% among targeted consumers by December 31, 2021; increase the number of 
retweets by followers by 5% among existing and new twitter followers by December 31, 2024).
List a GOAL for your organization: increase sales during COVID-19 
List at least one OBJECTIVE that relates to that goal for your organization: 
integrate the idea of a robot realtor by Jan. 1, 2021 to increase sales during COVID-19 by at 
least 3%. 
List a GOAL for your organization: increase new client traffic by 10% for the month of January 
2020
List at least one OBJECTIVE that relates to that goal for your organization: 
Increase new client traffic by 10% for the month of January, 2020, through segmented media 
coverage on various platforms (stickers on the ground about what we are doing about the 
pandemic; create a pamphlet to mail and email it to the group’s entire Rolodex, tenant offices 
for Minneapolis Skyrise buildings, beginning January 1, 2020.
List a GOAL for your organization: set the BBG apart from other competitors because of its 
response to the pandemic 
List at least one OBJECTIVE that relates to that goal for your organization: 
create a crisis message that highlights the value that the BBG places on the safety of its clients 
during the pandemic in order to set the BBG apart from other competitors because of its 
response at the time, beginning January 1, 2020.
Using at least 1 powerpoint slide, describe your goals and objectives for this campaign. You may 
use more than one slide if you would like.
