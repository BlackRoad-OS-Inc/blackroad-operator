What's prompting you to look for a new position?

I am looking to get back into advising after my wholesaling position. As a wholesaler, I worked almost exclusively with other advisors, and although I did make many great connections with them, I found myself missing the more personal connections  that I was able to make when working with clients directly. Financial advising also allows for a more holistic and involved approach to financial planning, whereas my experience with wholesaling was focused solely on annuity products.

Please describe how your past and/or current work experience relates to this position.

As an internal annuity wholesaler, I was the person that the financial advisors came to when they had questions about our products. While in that role, I gained invaluable experience deconstructing complex products and illustrations into easy-to-understand language that the advisors could utilize to help their clients come to decisions. Prior to my wholesaling position, I worked in the Ameriprise Advisor Center fielding client calls as I worked towards my licenses, then moved into an advising role where I could answer the questions that those clients had. In some ways, I have seen the financial industry from both sides, so I have a unique understanding that I believe will be very valuable to clients I advise in the future.
