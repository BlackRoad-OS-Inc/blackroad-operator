CASE STUDIES: CPC
CHALLENGE
How do you disrupt a category in the 
hyper growth market of cell & gene 
therapy? CPC, the world’s #1 brand of 
fluid connectors, had a new small-
format, aseptic single-use connector 
ready to launch globally. As the only 
state-of-the-art micro connector that 
reduces the risks associated with the 
status quo (tube welding) in biopharma 
process engineering, the stage was set 
for disruption.

## Page 17

CASE STUDIES: CPC
STRATEGY
To steal market share from existing  
tube-welding leaders, MK developed a bold 
message that intrigued customers to inquire 
about “the end of the weld.” Touting the category 
benefits of small format connectors vs. category 
risks associated with tube-welding, the 
messaging needed a bold creative introduction 
to drive inquiry and trial adoption.

## Page 18

CASE STUDIES: CPC
SOLUTION
A highly integrated big idea called “The End of 
the Weld” presented the market with a new way 
of connecting tubes in the sterile bio-pharma 
market.  
In phase one, advertising drove viewers to an 
engaging micro-site prompting them to sign up 
to be “the first to know.” This created an email 
database of potential new customers. Digital 
tools that supported product introductions and 
benefits were key engagement features on the 
site.

CASE STUDIES: 3M MED TECH
CHALLENGE
It can be slow and overwhelming for 
design engineers in the med tech space 
to research adhesive materials by 
themselves when they’re unsure of what 
they really need. This poses a risk to the 
success of their device and increases 
design and approval time.

## Page 25

CASE STUDIES: 3M MED TECH
STRATEGY
The client has proven success in recommending 
the optimal adhesive solutions by using a 
consultative approach. However, they needed a 
new way to communicate their expertise among 
a global audience. Together, the client and 
Morsekode created a product selector tool that 
helps visitors find the adhesive they need, offers 
education and samples, and ultimately drives 
leads. Morsekode then helped bring the right 
audience to the right place with paid media 
campaigns.

## Page 26

CASE STUDIES: 3M MED TECH
SOLUTION
The product selector tool helps engineers narrow-
in on adhesive solutions based on a series of 
questions. The tool uses a complex decision tree 
to match specific products to the audience’s 
needs. It then drives the user to connect with an 
expert for further discussion and product 
samples. This tool has been replicated on the 
client’s platform to better integrate the tool with 
other pages on the site and increase site 
engagement. 
Morsekode crafted a series of paid media 
campaigns in Google and LinkedIn to reach the 
target audience, drive traffic to the product 
selector tool, and generate high-quality leads.

## Page 27

CASE STUDIES: 3M MED TECH
RESULTS
The paid media campaigns launched in early 2018 using 
only the English language across 9 countries. They have 
since expanded to a total of 22 countries and 14 different 
languages.
These campaigns have successfully generated leads, filled 
3M’s sales pipeline, and have continued to improve over 
time. In the first four months of 2020, the campaigns 
generated 41% more opportunities than they did in all of 
2018 when they were first launched. This is due to an 
expanded global effort and continuous campaign 
optimization. The campaigns currently have a 12:1 ratio of 
opportunity value to ad spend. 
The Morsekode team uses performance-based insights to 
continuously improve campaigns. Some of these recent 
insights have led to greater cost-efficiency of the ads. 
When comparing the end of 2019 with the start of 2020, 
the cost per click (CPC) dropped 23%, driving traffic at a 
more cost-effective rate, and the cost per lead (CPL) 
dropped 37%
