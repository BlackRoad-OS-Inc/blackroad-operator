### **Why Consider Premier Protector?**

Premier Protector is a **variable annuity** that offers clients a **strong combination of growth potential, guaranteed death benefits, and tax efficiency**. Key benefits include:

✔ **Non-Qualified Stretch for Legacy Planning** – Clients can **spread inherited distributions over their lifetime**, avoiding a **large immediate tax hit** and keeping funds invested for continued growth. Distributions are taxed as **ordinary income instead of capital gains**, ensuring extended tax deferral.

✔ **Premier Protector Death Benefit** – The death benefit is the **greater of:**

- **Highest Anniversary Value** – The highest contract value on any anniversary (adjusted for purchase payments).

- **4% Increase Value** – Purchase payments grow at **4% annually** through age 85.

✔ **Investment Flexibility with Fidelity & American Funds Subaccounts** – Clients have access to **diverse investment options** across **large-cap, small/mid-cap, fixed income, and international markets** with competitive fees.

✔ **Accelerated Death Benefit for Chronic/Terminal Illness** – If clients qualify due to a chronic or terminal illness, they can **access the death benefit value while still living**, avoiding surrender charges.
