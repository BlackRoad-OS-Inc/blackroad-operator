## Slide 2

Getting Started
How to replace an image:  Right click over the image. Select “Replace Image” and select from listed upload options.
How to reposition an image: Left click to select photo then select the crop tool icon        and with cursor, reposition the photo inside the selected crop frame.
Scale up/scale down an image: Select photo then select the crop tool icon and while crop frame is selected hold down with your cursor on one of the blue squares of the photo and drag to expand or shrink the photo.
