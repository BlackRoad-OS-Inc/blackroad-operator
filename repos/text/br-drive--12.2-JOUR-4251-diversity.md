Socialization in families
Children impart basic values and modes of behavior consistent with the 
culture
Moral and religious principles, interpersonal skills, dress and grooming 
standards, appropriate manners and speech, selection of suitable 
educational and occupational or career goals
Consumer socialization
The process by which children acquire the skills, knowledge, attitudes, 
and experiences necessary to function as consumers
