Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis is a disease that destroys the nervous system and gradually causes physical disability. More commonly known to us as ALS, or Lou Gherig’s Disease, it affects approximately 20,000 people in the United States alone. Jenifer Estess was a young businesswoman with aspirations of marriage and a family of her own. However, those dreams quickly took a detour when she was diagnosed with ALS at the age of 35. Rather than spending her last days grieving, Jenifer helped raise $17 million for her foundation, project ALS, made popular recently by the ice bucket challenge. In her final moments ,Jennifer forces us to ask ourselves, when death stares us in the face, how will we be remembered? Tales from the Bed: A Memoir, by Jenifer Estess.

According to CBS News, the number of mothers who have decided to not return to work so they can stay at home to raise their children has increased by 15% since 2001. It seems that more and more families are finding it hard to balance both work and our society’s expectations of motherhood. Authors Illick and Krier examine this issue as Allison, a new mother and college professor, struggles to find this elusive balance between her dream job and raising a family.
