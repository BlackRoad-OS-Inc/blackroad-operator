**Subject:** What to Expect After Your Annuity Application is Submitted

Hi [Advisor's Name],

I wanted to share a quick overview of what happens after your broker-dealer submits an annuity application to Securian, so you know exactly what to expect and how to avoid delays:

**Application Review & Processing**

- Once we receive the application, our Annuity Services team reviews all forms for completeness and accuracy.

- If anything is Not In Good Order (NIGO), a missing requirements email will be sent to the case manager or financial professional.

- To help prevent delays, here are a few common issues to double-check:

• **Licensing & Appointment Status** – Ensure both are completed before the application is signed. If not, new signatures will be required.

• **Consistent Dates** – The application, replacement form(s), and 1035 Exchange/Transfer/Rollover form must all be signed with the same date.

• **Annuity Disclosure Statement** – If your broker-dealer does not perform a suitability review, this form is required and must be fully completed.

**Fund Transfers & Processing**

- If funds are not included with the application, we will request them from the existing carrier once all paperwork is in good order.

- If funds are included upfront, they will be applied to the contract immediately upon review.

- Our Annuity New Business team follows up regularly with the existing carrier until funds are received.

**Follow-Up Timing Based on Transfer Type**

- **Client-Initiated Transfers**: Follow-up with you on day 30 and every 30 days thereafter.

- **Carrier Processing Delays**: If the carrier requires client action or refuses to share information, we’ll reach out to coordinate next steps.

- **Check Submissions**: If the client submits a check, we’ll follow up every 30 days after the application is in good order.

- **Important**: Applications are held for a maximum of 6 months if funds are not received.

**Contract Issuance & Delivery**

- Once funds are applied, the contract is issued and prepared for delivery.

- Per your broker-dealer’s selling agreement, contracts are mailed to one of the following:

• The contract holder

• The financial professional

• The Broker-Dealer/IMO/Agency

- After the contract is issued:

• The contract owner will receive an Account Confirmation under separate cover.

• You can view the confirmation online in the **Statements and Confirms** section of your Book of Business.

If you have any questions about this process or want help navigating any step, please don’t hesitate to reach out. I’m here to help!
