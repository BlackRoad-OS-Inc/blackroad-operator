---

### **Yes, your client can move funds into a Securian annuity via Rollover, Transfer, or 1035 Exchange by following these steps.**

**You can support the process by:**

---

### **Step 1: Confirm the type of movement needed**

- **Rollover:** Moving between **different account types** (e.g., 401(k) to IRA)

- **Transfer:** Moving between **same account types** (e.g., IRA to IRA)

- **1035 Exchange:** Moving a **non-qualified annuity, life policy, or endowment** into a **non-qualified annuity**

Ask:

- “Is this qualified or non-qualified money?”

- “Has the original annuity been annuitized?”

- “Do the account types match?”

---

### **Step 2: Verify contract registration**

- Make sure the **owner(s)** and **annuitant(s)** on the Securian application match the current carrier.

- A **1035 Exchange** must be a **“like-to-like”** match (same registration).

If not, the request may be **rejected** by the ceding carrier.

---

### **Step 3: Check the ceding carrier’s requirements**

Ask the current carrier:

- Do they require their **own forms**?

- Do they need a **Medallion Signature Guarantee**?

- Do they require an **officer’s signature** from Securian?

- What is their **transfer processing address**?

Use this info to correctly complete the transfer paperwork.

---

### **Step 4: Complete and submit the paperwork**

Include:

- **1035 Exchange/Transfer/Rollover form** (required if Securian is requesting funds)

- **Application packet** with proper registration

- **Copy of the client’s current statement**

Optional (if required):

- Carrier-specific 1035 forms

- Medallion Signature Guarantee

- Certification of Trustee Authority or entity paperwork

---

### **Step 5: Submit paperwork to Securian**

Use the same submission options as with a new application:

**Mail:**

Securian Financial – Annuity Services

PO Box 64628

St. Paul, MN 55164-0628

**Fax:** 651-665-7942

**Email (new business):** idocannuity@securian.com

---

### **Step 6: Track the request status**

- Use the **Annuity Pending Business Tool** on [securianadvisor.com](https://www.securianadvisor.com)

- Check for updates, delays, or missing requirements

- Securian follows up with the ceding carrier every **30 days**

If the client initiated the transfer themselves, follow up with them directly.

---

### **Step 7: Understand timing limits**

- Securian holds incomplete transfer apps for **6 months**

- If the ceding carrier provides a delay window or requests client involvement, follow up to prevent cancellation

---

### **Step 8: Avoid common issues**

- Don’t attempt a 1035 exchange on an **annuitized contract**

- Always verify that the **registration is an exact match**

- Don’t forget to **document the replacement rationale** to meet compliance

### **Servicing the Contract**

This includes address changes, allocations, rebalancing, ownership updates, and more.

---

### **Yes, your client can make changes to their annuity contract after it's issued.**

**You can support the process by following the steps below:**

---

### **Step 1: To update the client’s address**

Your client can:

- Call Annuity Services directly

- Submit a **Service Request form**

- Send a signed letter requesting the change

- Update it themselves on **My Account**

You can:

- Call or email **Annuity@securian.com**

- Update it online through your **Book of Business** view

---

### **Step 2: To change future allocation of contributions** *(for variable annuities)*

Your client can:

- Call Annuity Services

- Log in online to update allocations

- Submit a **Future Allocation Change Request form**

You can:

- Call Securian to process it for the client

- Submit the same form on their behalf

---

### **Step 3: To set up or modify Automatic Purchase Plans (APP)**

Your client can:

- Use the **Automatic Payment Plan Request form**

- Set up monthly recurring contributions from a bank account

- Update or cancel at any time

You can:

- Help complete the APP form

- Submit it with routing/account info or help the client enter it online

---

### **Step 4: To set up Automatic Portfolio Rebalancing (APR)** *(variable annuities)*

Your client can:

- Complete the **Automatic Rebalancing Request form**

- Choose frequency and allocations

- Submit changes online or by form

You can:

- Assist with form completion or log in to adjust via the advisor portal

---

### **Step 5: To start or adjust Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)**

Your client can:

- Complete a **DCA Request form** to spread contributions over time

- Choose which subaccounts to fund and how often

You can:

- Recommend DCA as a volatility mitigation strategy

- Submit the form with the application or later

---

### **Step 6: To set up Systematic Withdrawals** *(for deferred annuities)*

Your client can:

- Complete the **Systematic Withdrawal Request form**

- Elect amount, frequency, start date, and tax withholding

You can:

- Help identify how systematic withdrawals can provide income or satisfy RMDs

- Submit the form and monitor the first withdrawal date

---

### **Step 7: To transfer between sub-accounts** *(for variable annuities)*

Your client can:

- Submit a **Sub-Account Transfer Request form**

- Call Annuity Services to process over the phone

You can:

- Assist by reviewing fund options and processing the change

- Note: Only **one transfer per year** is allowed **from the General Account**, and it must be ≤20% of that account’s value

---

### **Step 8: To change a beneficiary**

Your client can:

- Submit a **Beneficiary Change Request form**

- Provide full name, relationship, and percentages (not dollar amounts)

You can:

- Double-check class (Primary or Contingent) and per stirpes if desired

- Remind clients: **Estate is the default** if no beneficiary is named

---

### **Step 9: To update owner or annuitant information**

**For Owner/Annuitant Changes (non-qualified only):**

- Submit an **Owner/Annuitant Change Request form**

- Include **Certification of Trustee Authority** if the new owner is a trust

- All current owners, trustees, and new owners must sign

**For Name Changes (owner or annuitant):**

- Submit a **Service Request form**

- Attach proof (marriage cert, divorce decree, court order, etc.)

For one-time contributions:

- Use the **Additional Contribution Form** to designate subaccount and tax year

**Accepted:**

- Cashier’s/Certified checks with preprinted name

- Cash

- Credit cards

- Money orders

- Starter checks

- Checks signed by the advisor

- Traveler’s checks

- Third-party checks

- Checks made out to two parties for one client

- Foreign checks from banks without U.S. affiliations

You can:

- Double-check checks before submitting

- Avoid delays by flagging non-compliant payment types

### **Death Claim Procedures and Options**

### **Yes, your client’s beneficiary can access the annuity proceeds after death.**

**You can support the process by following these steps:**

---

### **Step 1: Notify Securian of the death**

You can:

- Call **1-800-362-3141** or email **AnnuityClaims@Securian.com**

- Provide the following details:

- Name and date of death of the annuitant or owner

- Contract number(s)

- Date of birth of the deceased

- Names, addresses, and contact info for the beneficiary(ies)

Securian will:

- Cancel any **APP or systematic withdrawal plans**

- Send a claim kit with settlement options to the beneficiary

- Copy you on all correspondence

---

### **Step 2: Confirm what type of contract it is**

You can:

- Determine whether the contract was **deferred** or **annuitized**

- Understand that:

- For **deferred contracts**, the **owner’s death** usually triggers the benefit

- If the owner is an entity (e.g., trust), the **annuitant’s death** triggers the benefit

- For **annuitized contracts**, death benefit depends on the income option selected

---

### **Step 3: Help the beneficiary choose a payout option**

You can review the available options below and explain the implications:

#### **For deferred annuities:**

- **Lump-sum payout** – Taxable immediately; paid by check or EFT

- **Annuitize the contract** – Converts to a payment stream; can reduce tax hit

- **Spousal continuation (treat as own)** – Spouse takes over the contract ownership

- **Spousal rollover to IRA** – No tax event; 1099-R issued for reporting only

- **Inherited/Beneficial account** – Retains tax deferral but RMDs are required

- Non-qualified: Must begin by **1 year from death**

- Qualified: Must begin by **December 31 of the year following death**

---

### **Step 4: Submit the required documentation**

You and/or the beneficiary must provide:

- **Legible death certificate**

- **Beneficiary Claim Request form**

Depending on situation, also submit:

- **Proof of age** if choosing life income option

- **Trust certification** (if trust is beneficiary)

- **Executor/Administrator papers** (if estate is beneficiary)

- **Transfer paperwork** if funds are moving to another carrier

- **Deceased beneficiary’s death cert** (if contingent beneficiaries are now primary)

---

### **Step 5: Support the claim through processing**

- Once all paperwork is received, Securian processes within **5 business days**

- Beneficiaries may:

- Reallocate variable funds (requires consent of all beneficiaries)

- Choose payout methods allowed by the contract and current tax law

- You’ll be copied on all beneficiary communications

---

### **Step 6: Avoid escheatment**

If the beneficiary fails to respond or claim proceeds:

- Securian will attempt to contact them

- If contact is lost and no action is taken, the proceeds may be sent to the **state as unclaimed property**

You can:

- Stay in touch with the beneficiary

- Ensure they complete all claim steps promptly to avoid delays or forfeiture

### **Distributions**

### **Yes, your client can take money from their annuity contract through several distribution options.**

**You can support the process by understanding what type of annuity they have and guiding them through the right steps:**

---

### **Step 1: Identify the type of distribution**

Ask:

- Is the annuity **deferred** or already **annuitized**?

- Is this a **systematic withdrawal**, **required minimum distribution (RMD)**, **partial surrender**, or **full surrender**?

Each path has its own requirements and consequences.

---

### **Step 2: Immediate annuitization (for guaranteed income)**

Your client can:

- Annuitize the contract to receive **guaranteed income payments**

You can support by submitting:

- **Application** signed by owner(s) and annuitant(s)

- **Annuity Quote** (must match application and be within quote window)

- **Payment & Withholding Instructions**

- **Proof of age** (e.g., driver’s license) for life-based payout options

**Note:**

- Annuitization is **permanent**—it cannot be reversed

- Systematic plans are automatically cancelled once annuitization occurs

---

### **Step 3: Systematic Withdrawals from a Deferred Annuity**

Your client can:

- Take recurring withdrawals for income or to satisfy RMDs

- Choose amount, frequency, and start date

You can support by:

- Submitting the **Systematic Withdrawal form**

- Monitoring withdrawal value and schedule

- Helping the client understand potential **charges**:

- **CDSC** (Contingent Deferred Sales Charge)

- **Federal tax penalties** (10% if under age 59½)

- **Market Value Adjustment** (MVA) for certain fixed contracts

---

### **Step 4: Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs)**

For qualified contracts, your client must take RMDs starting at their required beginning date.

You can:

- Use the **RMD Systematic Withdrawal form** to automate distributions

- Use Securian’s **RMD Calculator** or check the **RMD Notification Report** in your Book of Business

- Choose **Single Life** or **Joint Life** tables based on the client’s spouse

- Remind clients:

- First RMD is due **April 1 of the year after RBD**

- Future RMDs are due **December 31 each year**

---

### **Step 5: One-time Partial or Full Surrenders**

Your client can:

- Submit a **Partial Surrender Request form** or **Full Surrender Request form**

You can support by:

- Verifying the **plan administrator signs** (if 457, QRP, or 403(b))

- Preparing them for:

- **CDSC**

- **MVA**

- **IRS 10% penalty if under 59½**

- **20% mandatory withholding** for TSAs and some public plans

---

### **Step 6: Understand tax reporting (Form 1099-R)**

You can advise that:

- Most distributions result in a **1099-R** issued by January 31

- Reporting varies based on plan type (e.g., Securian reports for IRAs, but employers report for QRPs)

### **Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs)**

### **Yes, your client can meet their IRS-required distributions from a Securian annuity.**

**You can support the process by helping them plan, calculate, and set up their RMDs correctly.**

---

### **Step 1: Determine if the client is subject to RMD rules**

Ask:

- Is the annuity a **qualified contract** (e.g., Traditional IRA, SEP, SIMPLE, 403(b), 457, QRP)?

- Has the client reached their **Required Beginning Date (RBD)?**

**RBD** is typically **April 1 of the year after the client turns the applicable RMD age** (currently 73).

You can check:

- The **RMD Notification Report** on your Book of Business

- The **client’s annual statement**, which includes RMD messaging

---

### **Step 2: Choose a distribution method**

Your client can:

- Take **manual withdrawals** each year

- Set up a **Systematic Withdrawal Plan** to automate it

- Complete the **Telephone Redemption Authorization form** for call-in withdrawals

You can:

- Recommend a **systematic option** to avoid missed deadlines and IRS penalties

- Submit the **RMD Systematic Withdrawal Request form** to automate distributions

---

### **Step 3: Calculate the correct amount**

You can:

- Use the **RMD Calculator** on [securianadvisor.com](https://www.securianadvisor.com)

- Choose the appropriate **life expectancy method**:

- **Single Life Expectancy**: most common

- **Joint Life Expectancy**: only if spouse is sole beneficiary and >10 years younger

Remind clients:

- The IRS expects **each contract type to be treated separately**, but **like-plan aggregation** is allowed

- Example: Multiple IRAs can be aggregated, but IRAs and 403(b)s cannot

---

### **Step 4: Submit the RMD and verify withholding elections**

You can:

- Submit the **RMD form** with payment method, frequency, and tax withholding preferences

- Guide the client on whether to withhold federal/state taxes based on expected liability

---

### **Step 5: Monitor annual distribution compliance**

You can:

- Track client RMDs each year using your **Book of Business RMD report**

- Help avoid the **50% IRS penalty** for missed RMDs

- Work with the client in Q4 to make last-minute catch-ups if needed

---

### Summary of Key RMD Reminders:

- First RMD due **April 1** after reaching RBD

- All future RMDs due **by December 31** annually

- **Spouses can delay** RMDs if they inherit and treat the contract as their own

- For inherited accounts, distributions must begin within 1 year (or 10-year rule may apply)
