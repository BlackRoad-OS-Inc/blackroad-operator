## Slide 9

What is the problem we’re trying to solve?
Chobani as a brand is doing well, but the yogurt drinks are under-performing.
We need to shift the current mindset to create a more favorable association with Chobani drinkable yogurt.

## Slide 12

What does this audience already think?
Pleasure points
Product is “creamy, delicious, filling, cute, convenient”
74.% of respondents believe the product is of very high or extremely high quality
Pain points
Appealing to college-aged consumers
33 responses suggested making the product more affordable
10 responses suggesting special promotions for college students like free samples, coupons, campus ambassadors, etc.
Convenience/Availability
7 respondents suggested placing the product in vending machines, convenience stores, and other grab-and-go spots close to campus
No strong brand associations other than “yogurt” or “healthy”
(Google Survey. October 2018.)
