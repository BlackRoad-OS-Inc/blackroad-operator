Dean's List
Each semester, we publicly recognize our students'
superior academic performance through the
College of Liberal Arts dean's list.
Students on the dean's list will receive:
a notation on their transcripts
an emailed letter from the dean
their name posted publicly on the College of
Liberal Arts website (see below)
their name listed on an interactive digital display
outside of CLA Student Services, 16 Johnston
Hall (ground floor).
Please note: Students who have requested to
suppress all directory information will not have their
names included on the dean's list roster published to
the web or digital monitors.
Dean's List Requirements
To appear on the College of Liberal Arts dean's list,
you must:
complete at least 12 credits (including College of
Continuing Education and Professional Studies
credits) of A/F registration
earn a semester GPA of at least 3.666
receive no N grades or incompletes
For questions about the College of Liberal Arts
dean's list, contact the Oﬀice of Undergraduate
Education, 115 Johnston Hall, 612-624-8675.
Congratulations to everyone who earned a place on
the list! #UMNProud
