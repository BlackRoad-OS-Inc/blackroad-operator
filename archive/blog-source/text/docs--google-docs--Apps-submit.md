### **How to Submit Your SecureLink Ultra Application**

- **LPL & Osaic Advisors:** You **must** submit the application through your specific AOE platform.

- **Cetera Advisors:** You may **not** yet have access to the AOE platform, as it is currently in a pilot phase.

### **Next Steps After Submission**

✔ **Application Submission & Forwarding** – The application is forwarded to Securian’s Annuity Services for processing.

✔ **Application Review** – Annuity Services reviews all submitted forms to ensure they are in good order (EIGO). If anything is missing, Securian will send a missing requirement email to you.

✔ **Licensing & Appointment Verification** – Securian confirms that all product training, continuing education, and state-specific requirements (including NAIC Best Interest Training) were completed **before** the application was signed. If not, new signatures may be required.

✔ **Suitability Review (if applicable)** – If your broker-dealer is not handling the suitability review, Annuity Services ensures compliance. A completed **Annuity Disclosure Statement** is required.

✔ **Funds Request & Transfer Processing** – Once the application is in good order, Securian will initiate a **1035 exchange, transfer, or rollover request** if applicable. Follow-ups will be conducted with the current carrier to track fund movement.

✔ **Contract Issuance & Delivery** – Once all requirements are met and funds are received, the annuity contract will be issued and sent to the contract holder, financial professional, or broker-dealer, depending on the firm’s selling agreement.

✔ **Confirmation & Online Access** – The contract owner will receive a **separate Account Confirmation**.

You can track the application’s status using the **Annuity Pending Business Tool** on the advisor portal.

Let me know if you have any questions or need assistance with outstanding items. I’ll continue to monitor and provide updates as needed!
