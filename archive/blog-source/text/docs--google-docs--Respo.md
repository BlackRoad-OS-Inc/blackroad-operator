Re: Response to July 9, 2025 Cease-and-Desist Letter from BlackRock Finance, Inc. — Rebrand & Coexistence Proposal

Dear Ms. Martins:

BlackRoad Inc. (“BlackRoad”) acknowledges receipt of the July 9, 2025 cease-and-desist letter issued by Day Pitney LLP on behalf of BlackRock Finance, Inc. (“BlackRock”). We assert that “BlackRoad” does not infringe, but to address concerns, we offer to rebrand the word mark to **“Black Road”** and adopt **“Black Road Inc.”** as our public-facing name.

---

### Key Facts

1. **Independent Creation.**

- “BlackRoad” was selected to represent a path in decentralized finance, distinct from “BlackRock.” Changing to “Black Road” enhances differentiation with a space.

2. **Expressive Branding & Good Faith Filing.**

- The term “BlackRoad” was conceived independently by Alexa Amundson and reflects the founder’s journey into AI-native decentralized finance. Our branding, potentially incorporating the golden ratio for aesthetic harmony, follows expressive principles outlined in *Matal v. Tam* (582 U.S. 218, 2017) and *Jack Daniel’s v. VIP Products* (143 S. Ct. 1578, 2023), filed in good faith with no intent to trade on BlackRock’s reputation.

3. **Trademark Status.**

- U.S. apps **99161422** (word) and **99166452** (design) filed 29 Apr 2025, with 99161422 published (per unofficial notice). We will update the word mark to **“Black Road”** and rebrand to **“Black Road Inc.”**

4. **Distinct Branding & Services.**

- Logo: gradient “B” with road motif; word mark “Black Road,” corporate name “Black Road Inc.”

- Services: AI blockchain advisory, RoadChain, Roadcoin, non-IPO fundraising—separate from BlackRock Finance’s debt issuance.

5. **Established Use & Investment.**

- Domains: blackroadinc.us, blackroad.io; social media: @meetblackroad (X), @blackroadio (X), @blackroadinc (Instagram), @blackroad.io (Instagram).

- Instagram post (29 Apr 2025), X post (8 Jul 2025), and billing records (#3801177363 for $275.82, #3730223063 for $81.84) show investment.

6. **Professional Credentials.**

- FINRA Series 7/63/65, SIE, Life & Health license, former Minnesota Realtor. Experience: Ameriprise, Securian, CDW. BlackRoad Inc. is a single-advisor RIA.

7. **Class 36 Purpose & Flexibility.**

- Class 36 supports my licenses and non-IPO crypto-staking. We are open to exploring Class 42 for AI/blockchain tech if it resolves concerns, but 36 is essential.

8. **Crowded Field & No Confusion.**

- ~70 “BLACK ROCK / BLACKROCK” marks by 18+ entities show distinction. The coexistence of “BLACKRO_” marks across financial sectors without incident supports distinction between “BlackRock” and “Black Road,” given our unique tech focus and stylization. Under DuPont factors, no confusion is likely due to consumer sophistication, logo differences, and distinct impression. Only three “BlackRoad” marks exist: our filings and a Chinese sticker company.

9. **Coexistence Precedent.**

- *BlackRock Inc. v. Black Rock Funding Corp.* (S.D.N.Y. 2019) settled with adjustments.

---

## Rebrand & Coexistence Proposal

1. **Name** – rebrand word mark to **“Black Road”**, corporate name to **“Black Road Inc.”**; retain domains.

2. **Logo** – use gradient “B” with road motif.

3. **Disclaimer** – display **“Not affiliated with BlackRock Finance, Inc. or BlackRock Inc.”**

4. **Monitoring** – address credible confusion reports promptly.

We respectfully assert no likelihood of confusion under U.S. trademark law. In cooperation, we have paused “BlackRoad” usage and are willing to implement the rebrand at significant cost, provided it resolves BlackRock’s concerns. This aligns with 15 U.S.C. § 1052(d) for coexistence where confusion is unlikely.

We welcome confirmation of this proposal’s acceptance and a path to peaceful coexistence. We are available for further discussion or documentation.
