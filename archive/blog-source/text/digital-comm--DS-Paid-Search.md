## Slide 9

Paid Search

Definition: form of digital marketing where search engines allow advertisers to show ads on their search engine results pages (SERPs).

Measured on a pay-per-click model where you only pay for the ad if someone clicks on it

Ad formats include text only ads, shopping ads which feature pictures of the product and call only ads where you pay per phone call.

Why it matters: you’re targeting an audience that is showing a real and current interest through their actions that they’re interested in awareness, consideration or conversion in the industry/product that you offer

## Slide 11

Keyword development

You may start with Google’s Keyword Planner.

Think about what you’d search to find this product.

Look at what ads and competitors are showing up when you search and see if there’s a niche that’s not being covered right now.


## Slide 12

How it works

Choose your geography.

Choose your keywords.

Exact match: pick up plural and spelling mistakes

Phrase match: can add words before or after your keywords

Modified broad match: phrase in any order without synonyms

Broad match: phrase in any order and with synonyms

Negative keywords: include in campaign to ensure you only appear in relevant searches. For example – “wakeboard dangers”

## Slide 15

Search Ad Considerations

Each ad should have a clear call to action, unique selling points (what is your unique value proposition?) and words that are relevant to the keyword word search.

You cannot use all caps, special symbols such as exclamation points or trademarked terms.


## Slide 16

Search Ad Consideration Continued

Ensure you’ve set up conversion tracking so you understand the outcome of each ad.

Only running broad match searches can limit your success.

Have a clearly defined goal that includes a measurement KPI.

Calculate the conversion rate from your ads to know if your budget can reach your goal.
