# Business Proposals

**Source:** google-docs

---

Step 1: Who are you?

What would be the future obituary for your brand?

Where does your passion lie?

Where do you have the most experience?

Where do you have the most credibility?

Step 2: What do you do?

What business are you in?

What is your purpose, beyond selling a product or service?

What is your purpose in 12 words or less?

Step 3: What’s your vision?

What would you draw or write - whatever, to paint a vivid picture of your future?

Test this on a real piece of communication.

What do you want to accomplish in 5, 10, or 20 years?

How could you make this vision palpable and exciting?

Could you go back and refine this further? If so, do it.

If no more revisions, use it repeatedly to illustrate the direction of your business.

Step 4: What wave are you riding?
