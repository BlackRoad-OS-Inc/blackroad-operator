Next Steps After Submission:

Application Submission & Forwarding

The application is forwarded to Securian’s Annuity Services for processing.

Application Review

Annuity Services reviews the submitted application and required forms to ensure everything is in good order.

If anything is missing, Securian will send a Missing Requirement Email to you.

Licensing & Appointment Verification

Securian confirms that all required training and compliance requirements were completed before the application was signed, including:

Product training

Continuing education

State-specific requirements (including NAIC Best Interest training)

If these were not completed in advance, new signatures may be required.

Suitability Review (if applicable)

If your broker-dealer does not handle suitability review, Annuity Services will review the case for compliance.

A completed Annuity Disclosure Statement is required.

Funds Request & Transfer Processing

Once the application is in good order, Securian will:

Initiate a 1035 exchange, transfer, or rollover request (if applicable).

Conduct follow-ups with the existing carrier to track fund movement.

Contract Issuance & Delivery

Once all requirements are met and funds are received, the annuity contract will be issued.

It will be sent to the contract holder, financial professional, or broker-dealer, depending on the firm's selling agreement.

Confirmation & Online Access

The contract owner will receive an Account Confirmation separately.

Tracking & Assistance:

You can track the application’s status using the Annuity Pending Business Tool on the advisor portal.

Let me know if you have any questions or need assistance with outstanding items.

I’ll continue to monitor and provide updates as needed!
