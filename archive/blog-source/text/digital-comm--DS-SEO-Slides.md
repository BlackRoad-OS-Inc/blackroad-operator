## Slide 18

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Definition: the process of improving your site to increase its visibility when people search for products or services

Onsite SEO is what you do to your website to make it easier for search engines to find it.

Offsite SEO includes the things you do outside of your site, besides advertising.

You can’t pay search engines to get higher organic search rankings.


## Slide 19

How it works – Onsite SEO

Search engines such as Google and Bing use bots to crawl pages on the web, going from site to site, collecting information about those pages and putting them in an index. Next, algorithms analyze pages in the index, considering hundreds of ranking factors or signals, to determine the order pages should appear in the search results for a given query. A webpage can be seen as the most relevant, least relevant and anywhere in-between when someone searches for a term.


## Slide 20

How it works – Offsite SEO

Link building is the primary tool of offsite SEO whereby links to your website appear on other websites.

This can be achieved though:

Other websites posting a press release that includes your webpage.

When either your own social media channels or others link to interesting articles, products and webpages.

Guest bloggers on your website who will then link to the post on your website on their website and promote the post on their social media channels.


## Slide 21

Why it matters and the bad news

Search is often the primary source of digital traffic for brands’ websites and complements other marketing channels. Greater visibility and ranking higher in search results than your competition can directly translate into higher revenue.

Conversely and unfortunately for brands, search results have been evolving over the past few years to give users more direct answers and information that is more likely to keep users on the results page instead of driving them to other websites.


## Slide 22

What’s included in a good SEO website strategy

The usage of keywords in several key places including:

Words in the URL. For example: resorts.com/Hawaii-beaches

Website title tags

Header tags found in the HTML of a website

The main body copy of a webpage aiming for 1-2% of the total words to be keywords. Past that point, search engines will downgrade your page in search ranking due to keyword overstuffing

Image alt text so search engines can “see” what images are

Linking to other relevant pages on your website within the main body copy


## Slide 23

What’s included in a good SEO website strategy

A sitemap that helps search engines easily understand the pages on your website

Contact information including a Google Map and actual photo if you have a storefront

Updating your blog and other webpages regularly.

Enabling comments

Short page load times

Mobile optimized

Strong security, search engines prefer https vs http.


## Slide 24

Local search strategies

In a local search, businesses should have a Google My Business profile and associate this profile with a well optimized landing page.

Many positive reviews will also increase your local search ranking.


## Slide 25

Building a good keyword strategy

The goal of SEO is not to rank highly for a single keyword or phrase but to have your website rank highly for many different keywords.

Example: furniture, sofa, couch, loveseat, sectional, table, chairs, recliner, dining room table, coffee table, etc.

Match broader keywords with higher level website pages and more specific keywords with lower-level website pages.

Example: Homepage: Jewelry, subpage: earrings, sub subpage: hoops, studs


## Slide 26

Building a good keyword strategy

The choosing of keywords should be based on:

Relevance: have a mix of generic and more specific, commercial terms

Traffic: Google’s Keyword Planner provides an estimate of the monthly search volume of a given phrase and related search phrases. SEO pros use more advanced tools.

Competition: if an industry giant has cornered the market in a generic keyword, try to use a more specific version of that word

Current ranking: Invest your efforts into the keywords that have the most relevance or traffic vs. spending time trying to prop up keywords with little organic traffic.
