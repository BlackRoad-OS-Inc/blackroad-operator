Why Consider MultiOption Momentum?

MultiOption Momentum offers a dynamic strategy for growth, risk management, and tax-efficient legacy planning. Clients benefit from:

Non-Qualified Stretch – Clients can spread inherited distributions over their lifetime, avoiding a large immediate tax hit and keeping funds invested. Distributions are taxed as ordinary income instead of capital gains, ensuring continued tax deferral.

Fidelity & American Funds Subaccounts – Providing diversified exposure to large-cap, small/mid-cap, fixed income, and international markets with competitive fees.

Fixed Indexed Accounts for Risk Reduction – Featuring the S&P 500® 1-Year Point-to-Point with Cap, allowing clients to gradually move funds into downside protection while still participating in market growth.

Tax-Free Transfers & No Fees on Indexed Amounts – Clients can reallocate assets from variable subaccounts to indexed accounts without incurring taxes and reduce fees since indexed accounts have no fees.

Hypothetical Risk Reduction Strategy

Imagine a client initially invests 100% of their funds into variable subaccounts. Over time, they gradually reduce risk using one of these strategies:

Locking in Gains Annually: Every contract anniversary, the client transfers investment gains from the variable subaccounts into the fixed indexed account, ensuring those gains are protected from market downturns while still growing with 100% downside protection.

Gradual Risk Reduction: Instead of moving only gains, the client transfers 10% of their total contract value every contract anniversary into the fixed indexed account, eliminating fees on that portion while maintaining downside protection. This strategy:

Gradually de-risks the portfolio over time

Keeps a portion of the contract secure while maintaining equity exposure

Ensures tax-free transfers between accounts

Maintaining Control Over Risk – Even while reducing risk, the client can remain invested in variable subaccounts with access to:

Government securities & money markets for stability

Bond funds for fixed income diversification

Equity funds for growth potential

This structure allows clients to manage their own risk exposure, ensuring their investment mix aligns with their retirement goals.
