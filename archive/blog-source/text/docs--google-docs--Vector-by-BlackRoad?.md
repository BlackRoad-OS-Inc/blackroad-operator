How AI functions in Cities: Skylines

Pathfinding:   Agents (vehicles and pedestrians) constantly calculate the most efficient path to their destination, considering various costs like time, distance, and lane changes.

* Accelerate or brake to match surrounding traffic.

* Make lane changes to optimize flow or avoid obstacles.

* React to unexpected events, such as rerouting due to an accident.

Inter-agent interaction:   Agents are aware of each other and their surroundings, which creates a more naturalistic and chaotic environment.

Complexity and realism:   The depth of the simulation aims to create the feeling of a living city, with countless individual decisions contributing to the overall order and chaos.

Cities: Skylines 2 (CS2): features a more advanced and deeper traffic simulation.

Better resource management: The AI is designed to account for various factors simultaneously, leading to more intelligent pathfinding and behavior.

They can teach real skills, like planning in SimCity or coding basics in Roblox, but may include repetitive tasks that not everyone finds engaging. For controversial aspects, such as monetization in Roblox or realism in simulators, sources show mixed opinions, with some praising immersion and others calling for better safeguards.

Recommendations

If seeking relaxation, try Animal Crossing or Stardew Valley. For building empires, SimCity or Minecraft fit well. Always check age ratings and updates, as games evolve—e.g., The Sims 4 went free-to-play in 2022.

The Survivalists stands as a prime example of a creative survival-crafting game that blends social elements with expansive sandbox freedom. Set in a procedurally generated wilderness within The Escapists universe, players embark on joint adventures in a living world that changes with day/night cycles. Core gameplay revolves around exploring diverse biomes to uncover secrets, gathering resources like food from hunting animals, and crafting a wide array of items—from primitive axes to advanced structures—using discovered recipes. A standout feature is the ability to train monkeys via the Mimic System, effectively recruiting them as NPCs to assist with tasks such as building, lumberjacking, combat support during raids on fanatic camps, or even helping in temple explorations for epic loot. This mechanic emphasizes community building, as monkeys become integral to survival and thriving, allowing players to create efficient settlements or villages without a prescribed narrative. Multiplayer supports up to three friends for cooperative play, including loot sharing and trading, fostering imaginative construction and collaboration. Quests from a Mysterious Stranger or washed-up items provide optional direction, but the game's purpose is open-ended survival and creativity, with no overarching story—players define their experience through building, crafting, and exploration. Released in 2020 by Team17, it has been praised for its humor, accessibility, and the joy of managing a monkey workforce, though some note repetition in tasks. Its appeal lies in the balance of danger (mythical enemies, terrifying foes) and cozy elements, making it ideal for players who enjoy emergent gameplay in a vibrant, surprise-filled island setting.store.steampowered.com

SimCity, the pioneering city-building simulation series created by Will Wright and first released in 1989 by Maxis (later acquired by Electronic Arts), serves as a virtual urban planning tool where players assume the role of a mayor to design, construct, and manage a city from scratch. The core purpose is to simulate realistic city development and management, drawing from urban planning theories like Jay Forrester's Urban Dynamics, allowing players to experiment with zoning, infrastructure, and policies in an open-ended environment. Gameplay involves starting with a blank map and initial budget, zoning land for residential, commercial, and industrial use, and providing essential services such as electricity, water, waste management, health, education, and safety—each with a limited range of influence to encourage strategic placement. Financial management is key, relying on taxation, optional deals like legalizing gambling or hosting military bases, and trading utilities with neighboring cities via connected infrastructure. Challenges include balancing budgets to avoid strikes or decline, responding to disasters like fires, tornadoes, or fictional monster attacks, and adapting to citizen needs for growth. Key features evolved across titles: the original used a dimetric view; SimCity 2000 (1993) added isometric graphics and underground layers; SimCity 3000 (1999) introduced waste deals; SimCity 4 (2003) featured regions and Sims integration; and the 2013 reboot brought 3D graphics and multiplayer regions, though plagued by launch issues. Educational aspects shine through its use in classrooms for teaching urban planning, though critics note oversimplifications, such as low taxes always spurring growth or police reducing crime uniformly. Realistic elements include simulating traffic, pollution, and economic ripple effects, making it a caricature rather than a precise model. The series has sold millions, inspiring games like Cities: Skylines, and spin-offs like SimTown, but development halted after Maxis's 2015 closure.en.wikipedia.org+3 more

Animal Crossing, a beloved social simulation series developed by Nintendo since 2001, created by Katsuya Eguchi and Hisashi Nogami, invites players to live a relaxed, open-ended life in a rural village inhabited by anthropomorphic animals. The point of the game is to provide a stress-free, charming escape where players set their own goals, such as paying off a mortgage, collecting items, decorating homes and islands, or building relationships, all at a leisurely pace. Core gameplay includes daily activities like gathering fruit, seashells, and fossils; crafting items; planting plants; fishing; catching insects; and socializing with villagers through conversations, letters, bartering, or mini-games like hide-and-seek. The series synchronizes with the console's real-time clock, simulating seasonal changes, holidays, and events—trees grow over days, and time-specific occurrences like night-time bugs add immersion. Customization is extensive: players design their character's appearance, home furnishings, village name, anthem, and some villager traits, with randomly generated terrain and residents adding uniqueness. There are no strict objectives, but subtle encouragements include expanding homes, donating to museums, or achieving island ratings in New Horizons (2020), which introduced island customization and villager recruitment. Appeal stems from its relaxing vibe, quirky humor, hourly music, and social features—players can visit friends' villages online or via memory cards, host events, or engage in virtual protests/weddings. It's empathetic to diverse playstyles, fostering joy in arbitrary goals like gardening, and has been a cultural phenomenon, especially during lockdowns, for its therapeutic qualities. While some find it too slow, its open-ended nature and community focus make it ideal for casual gamers.en.wikipedia.org+4 more

Stardew Valley, developed single-handedly by Eric "ConcernedApe" Barone and released in 2016, is a farm life simulation inspired by Harvest Moon, where players inherit a rundown farm in Pelican Town and work to restore it while revitalizing the community. The purpose is to offer an open-ended escape into rural life, blending farming, social interactions, and exploration with light RPG elements for a rewarding, therapeutic experience. Gameplay unfolds in a top-down world across four 28-day seasons, where players clear land, plant and water crops, raise animals in barns/coops for products like milk or eggs, and develop skills in farming, foraging, fishing, mining, and combat. Energy management is central—tasks deplete a daily bar, replenished by food—and players can mine in caves for ores, fight monsters, or complete quests. Objectives center on restoring the Community Center by collecting bundles of goods, unlocking areas like a desert or Ginger Island, though players can opt for a corporate path with JojaMart. Features include choosing farm types with unique benefits, multiplayer for cooperative play, and social elements like gifting to build relationships, leading to marriage (same-sex inclusive) and children, with spouses aiding farm chores. Appeal lies in its freedom—avoiding repetitive loops, integrating heartfelt stories, and anti-capitalist themes critiquing Joja Corporation—fostering immersion in a vibrant town with diverse NPCs. Praised for pixel art, music, and inclusivity, it has sold over 41 million copies by 2024, reviving the genre, though some note early grind. Educational value includes teaching planning and empathy, making it a cozy staple for relaxation and creativity.en.wikipedia.org+5 more

The Sims series, launched in 2000 by Maxis (under Electronic Arts since 1997) and inspired by creator Will Wright's post-disaster rebuilding experience, is a groundbreaking life simulation where players create and control virtual people ("Sims") to navigate daily lives, relationships, and aspirations in a sandbox environment. The purpose is to simulate real-life scenarios satirizing U.S. consumer culture, based on psychological models like Maslow's hierarchy and architectural theories, allowing open-ended storytelling without fixed goals—though expansions add missions. Gameplay mechanics involve customizing Sims' appearances, personalities, and homes using build/buy modes, managing needs (hunger, hygiene, social), wants/fears (introduced in The Sims 2), and life stages from infancy to death. Players direct moods, careers, and interactions, earning aspiration points for rewards in a real-time or accelerated simulation. Key features evolved: The Sims (2000) used dimetric views; The Sims 2 (2004) added 3D, aging, and genetics; The Sims 3 (2009) featured open neighborhoods and wishes; The Sims 4 (2014, free-to-play since 2022) offers expansions, game packs, and kits for new content like emotions or supernatural elements. Spin-offs include The Sims Online (multiplayer), The Sims Medieval (story-driven), and mobile versions with real-time progression. Uniqueness stems from its goal-less sandbox, enabling creative expression—build dream homes, simulate fantasies, or explore consequences—while expansions enrich with themes like pets or university life. It's sold nearly 200 million copies, generating $5 billion, praised for empathy-building but critiqued for monetization. Overall, it blends entertainment with subtle education on life choices.en.wikipedia.org+3 more

Roblox, founded in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel and released in 2006 by Roblox Corporation, is an online platform and game creation system that empowers users to program, share, and play millions of user-generated games, reimagining human connection through immersive 3D experiences. Its purpose is to build a collaborative ecosystem for play, learning, communication, and exploration, targeting a family-friendly audience with a focus on creativity and social interaction. Features include Roblox Studio, a free tool using Luau scripting for game development, with templates for easy creation—over 2 million developers, many under 18, produce 20 million+ games yearly across genres like role-playing and simulations. The virtual economy revolves around Robux currency for purchases, avatar customization, and premium access, with creators earning real money via the Developer Exchange (e.g., $250 million in 2020). Social aspects encompass chat, messaging, voice for verified users over 13, "Connections" for friends, and communities (formerly Groups) for discussions. Educational value is strong, teaching coding, game design, and entrepreneurship through self-directed projects and feedback from 85.3 million daily users (as of February 2025), half American kids under 16. The ecosystem thrives on user-generated content, including avatar items and "experiences" with age ratings, plus events like virtual concerts (Lil Nas X), protests, and brand partnerships (Chipotle). While praised for fostering skills and inclusivity, it faces criticism for safety issues, exploitation, and loot boxes, leading to bans in some countries. With 120 million monthly players during the pandemic and $2 billion+ mobile revenue, Roblox embodies a metaverse-like space for shared, dynamic interactions.en.wikipedia.org+5 more

Minecraft, developed by Markus "Notch" Persson and released in 2011 by Mojang Studios (acquired by Microsoft in 2014), is a 3D sandbox game emphasizing creative freedom in a procedurally generated, voxel-based world with no mandatory goals. Its purpose is to provide a platform for imagination, where players explore infinite terrains, extract materials, craft items, and build anything from simple shelters to complex machines, while optionally surviving threats. Game modes include Survival (gather resources, manage health/hunger, fight mobs); Creative (unlimited resources, flight, no dangers); Hardcore (permanent death); and Adventure (custom maps with restricted interactions). Core activities encompass mining blocks for materials like ores, crafting tools/armor via grids, building structures, farming, trading with villagers using emeralds, and using redstone for circuits. Exploration spans biomes, dimensions (Nether, End), and day/night cycles influencing mob spawns—passive for food, hostile like zombies at night. Goals in Survival involve optional achievements, boss fights (e.g., Ender Dragon for end credits and poem), but players can continue indefinitely. Creative freedom is unparalleled: modding adds mechanics, texture packs alter visuals, custom maps/servers enable multiplayer (direct, LAN, or hosted), and the Marketplace offers add-ons. With over 300 million copies sold by 2023, it's used educationally for problem-solving and coding, praised for emergent gameplay but noted for intensity in survival. Spin-offs like Minecraft Legends expand lore, solidifying its cultural impact.en.wikipedia.org+5 more

Simulation video games form a broad genre that replicates real-world activities, systems, or scenarios for entertainment, training, analysis, prediction, or education, often without defined goals to allow free exploration. Their purpose is to immerse players in realistic or hypothetical environments, fostering skills like decision-making, empathy, and critical thinking by mimicking life aspects in a risk-free setting. Subgenres include construction and management simulations (CMS) for building/managing communities with limited resources; life simulations for controlling artificial lives, ecosystems, or social interactions; sports simulations emphasizing gameplay or strategy (e.g., Madden NFL for action, Football Manager for tactics), sometimes satirical (e.g., Arch Rivals); medical simulations (e.g., Trauma Center for surgeries); photography simulations (e.g., Pokémon Snap); police simulations (e.g., LCPDFR for law enforcement); and military simulations (e.g., Arma series for realistic warfare). They replicate activities through detailed mechanics, such as resource management in CMS or team strategies in sports, blending with other genres like RPGs. Examples span war/business games to role-plays, used in education to enhance learning and attitudes. While praised for immersion and skill-building, they can oversimplify complexities, serving as hybrids of games and simulations.en.wikipedia.org+6 more
