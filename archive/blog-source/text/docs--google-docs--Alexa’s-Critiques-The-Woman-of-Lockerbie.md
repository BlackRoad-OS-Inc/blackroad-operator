Try doing your pantomiming in front of your mirror. Watch yourself so your miming makes sense and is even.

“Would I like to exchange this for something else?” How does she feel about this? Is she angry, is she sad?

Really internalize this character, what’s her life like? What’s her favorite color? What’s in her pocket? Work on knowing this character inside and out so you can become her.

This has real potential, keep working!
