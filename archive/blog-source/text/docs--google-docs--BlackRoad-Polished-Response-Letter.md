## Rebrand & Coexistence Proposal

1. **Name** – rebrand word mark to **“Black Road”**, corporate name to **“Black Road Inc.”**; retain domains.

2. **Logo** – use gradient “B” with road motif.

3. **Disclaimer** – display **“Not affiliated with BlackRock Finance, Inc. or BlackRock Inc.”**

4. **Monitoring** – address credible confusion reports promptly.

We respectfully assert that no likelihood of confusion exists under U.S. trademark law. However, in a gesture of cooperation and to avoid protracted conflict, we have voluntarily paused “BlackRoad” usage and are willing to implement the outlined rebrand at considerable expense, provided it meaningfully resolves BlackRock’s stated concerns. This offer comports with the spirit of cooperative resolution anticipated under 15 U.S.C. § 1052(d), which allows coexistence where confusion is unlikely based on mark differences, consumer sophistication, and market segmentation.

We welcome confirmation that this proposal satisfies BlackRock’s concerns and establishes a path forward for peaceful coexistence. Should you wish to discuss refinements or require further documentation, we remain available for dialogue.
