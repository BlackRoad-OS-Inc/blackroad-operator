BlackRoad Inc. (“BlackRoad”) acknowledges receipt of the July 9, 2025 cease-and-desist letter issued by Day Pitney LLP on behalf of BlackRock Finance, Inc. (“BlackRock”). Please disregard our earlier 4:01 PM response, which was sent in error. We assert with unshakable certainty that the “BlackRoad” mark does not infringe upon BlackRock’s rights, as no likelihood of confusion exists under the Lanham Act (15 U.S.C. § 1051 et seq.). In the spirit of good faith and to avoid unnecessary conflict, we propose a full rebrand to “Black Road” as the word mark and “Black Road Inc.” as our public-facing name and corporate identity, offered voluntarily and at our expense. We are also eager to collaborate with BlackRock to refine our logo, welcoming their input to ensure mutual satisfaction.
