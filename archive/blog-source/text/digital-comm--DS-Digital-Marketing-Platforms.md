## Slide 3

Social Media – Most people are already there

An average of 2 hours and 22 minutes are spent on social per person per day.

You can use organic and paid social media posts

If a person follows your brand, they’ve already indicated interest/consideration and likely have already purchased something from you

Advantages: it’s low cost, relatively easy to develop content for, you can take advantage of existing trends/hashtags, the right tone can go viral

Disadvantages: you can be taken over by trolls, organic content reach is dependent on the platform algorithms, the quick scroll means it’s likely people may miss your content


## Slide 4

Social Media – Creepy but effective targeting

Platforms allow for specific targeting:

Custom audiences – you can upload a list of your current customers and target them. Usually need to have a minimum of 2,500-5,000 customers.

If you want to reach only prospects, you can also upload a list of your customers and exclude them from targeting

Lookalike audiences – you use a custom audience and try to find prospective customers that look like your existing customers

Can target based on geography, interests, your physical location, your website browsing history


## Slide 5

Social Media – Customer service is key.

Nowadays, people consider your social media channels a prime way to get customer service. Some teams have a fulltime role dedicated to responding to customer complaints.

If you fail to respond to customer complaints, it can damage your brand. Especially if someone’s complaint goes viral.


## Slide 6

Influencer marketing – The ultimate brand ambassador

Most people on social media prefer to hear from people vs. brands.

Over time, influencers build up trust with their audiences, making them a more credible source for recommending a brand and its products and services.

Advantages: you reach people that may not follow your brand who trust an influencer’s recommendation increasing brand awareness

Disadvantages: it can be expensive, branded posts may not perform as well as the influencer’s usual organic content (especially if the brand doesn’t fit their usual style), an influencer may have fake followers, you lose some control of the content created about your brand


## Slide 7

Email marketing – from consideration to conversion

Stay connected with prospects and customers, sending them customized newsletters or offers based on past shopping history or brand engagements.

Frequently an offer for what may already be in someone’s online cart (not checked out) can be the nudge that’s needed to close a sale

Build up a list of subscribers by adding the emails of everyone who books through a website or submits a lead generation form

Advantages: low cost, send content tailored to specific interests

Disadvantages: can have low open rates


## Slide 8

Content marketing

Website content (blogs, articles), videos, and other assets can answer questions or provide context to consumers throughout the three stages of the buyer’s journey.

Visitors to your content marketing can come from many different channels: organic search, emails, social media posts with links/videos, YouTube

This is your chance to educate and inform customers and prospects about why you and your brand have what they need

Advantages: you control the content, it’s low cost

Disadvantages: bringing people to your content can be difficult, especially if there’s already a lot of similar content from competitors


## Slide 9

SEM – SEO – Search Engine Marketing – Content Marketing’s Cousin

Optimize content for the user experience and ensuring the technical elements are in place to enable search engine crawlers to easily find and index this content. This is done in part by filling in the content management system (CMS) meta data.

Advantages: Can increase traffic to your website at no cost.

Disadvantages: Can be time consuming, there can hard to develop unique keywords.


## Slide 10

SEM - PPC – Pay-Per-Click

Purchase traffic to your website.

How it works: place ads on websites or search engines such as Google and Bing, and pay a fee each time the ad is clicked on. These ads often appear at the top of the search results page, and are typically determined by bids on specific keywords.

Advantages: people are likely to click on one of the first things that are served up. May be blind to the word “ad”

Disadvantages: limited popular keywords so it can be very expensive


## Slide 11

Affiliate Marketing

Pay someone else to promote your brand or product in exchange for a commission from each sale that can be attributed to their efforts (For example – ESPN20)

Advantages: reach a unique audience that may not follow your brand. Outsource some of the heavy lifting of promotion.

Disadvantages: your brand's reputation is in someone else’s hands, so it often requires more extensive monitoring and tracking.


## Slide 12

Mobile Marketing

Includes many of the digital platforms mentioned already, and typically leverages a combination of text messages, social media, email, push notifications, and mobile applications.

As more people shop on mobile apps and websites, it’s important that all your content is designed to be mobile-first and accessible.


## Slide 13

Benefits of digital marketing platforms

1) Allows you test and learn from different platforms to see where your target audience is most receptive/active.

2) Allows you to compare platforms to see where you should focus your digital strategy efforts.

3) Multitouch attribution: you can connect all your digital content to a single media management platform and see which touchpoints are directly attributing to someone converting.
