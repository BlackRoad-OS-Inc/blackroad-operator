## Slide 6

What does PESO mean?

Paid: the channels in which money is paid to place the message, and control its distribution. This could be an ad or branded content.

Earned: the published coverage of an enterprise, cause or person's message by a credible third party, such as a journalist, blogger, trade analyst or industry influencer. You’re trading valuable content for an established authority’s audience.

A brand’s PR/communications team might pitch media and get a placement as result of that pitch/relationship. This would still be earned if no payment was exchanged.

Shared: Amplifying content through your own audience sharing and commenting on your social media content.

Owned: Building and maintaining an audience that seeks you out for content and then distributing your content to that audience through a blog, website or other channels.

You might use SEO to ensure that your owned content is easily searchable or PPC to bring your owned content to the top of a search.


## Slide 7

Why it matters:

It’s the big-picture, all-channels, inclusive way to communicate and earn attention. Demonstrates that there’s more than one way to strategically get a message out there.

Strategic use of ALL the PESO channels means you can share your message with your key stakeholders, customers and prospects when and where they choose to engage.

If you only focus on one area, you’re leaving progress against your goals on the tables, whether that’s revenue or awareness of your cause.

It’s the intersection of PR, digital, social media, website content and thought leadership that drives engagement as well as awareness, consideration and conversion.


## Slide 8

What this looks like: Explore Minnesota

Paid: We want to raise awareness of Explore Minnesota. We pay to be featured in a sponsored content piece on best fall road trips on travelandleisure.com or we pay an influencer to visit MN and highlight all the great things about the state.

Earned: The Chicago Tribune travel section includes Minnesota and links back to Explore MN a “Top places to road trip this fall” article. They did this because they really do believe it’s a great place to visit, we didn’t incentivize this coverage.

Shared: Followers share Explore Minnesota content on their own Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest to promote visiting the state

Owned: Explore Minnesota uses its frequently updated website to inform visitors about all the great things the state has to offer.

To add a layer of messiness, within a website like Explore Minnesota, we could have paid content from various hotels, restaurants and attractions.
