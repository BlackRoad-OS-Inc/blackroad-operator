# Financial analyst

**Source:** google-docs

---

Analyze actual results at the corporate and division levels

Solidify and inspect budgets and income statement forecasts

Generate ingenious alternatives and ideas to reduce costs and grow financial performance of a company

Partake in the planning of monthly financial reporting and other business data

Partake in ensuring adequate controls and procedures

Possibly explain complex financial subjects to non-financial staff members in special projects

Research and query large amounts of data

Cooperate with internal and external auditors during financial and operational audits

Partake in annual budgeting process

Support multiple aspects of the monthly/quarterly/annual close process (i.e. journal entries, account reconciliations, expense analysis, and trend analysis, variance analysis)

Tend to monitoring and maintaining internal accounting controls to ensure compliance with Sarbanes Oxley requirements
