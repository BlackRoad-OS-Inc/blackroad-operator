What you want. Can be abstract or more speciﬁc.
Be happy, be healthy, buy food for dinner

●
Desirable
●

●

## Page 12

Goals
Behavioral intention: get food for dinner
Implementation intention (how?): purchase these three items from a 
particular supermarked at 4 PM today
Focal goal: actively pursue + consciously aware
Background goal: might inﬂuence goal pursuit, but not explicitly aware of it 
at that time

## Page 13

Goals in buying
●
Utilitarian goals: things you need; practical. I need toothpaste so I buy 
toothpaste
●
Self-expression goals: I wear designer clothes
People differ individually on whether they buy stuff for utilitarian or 
self-expression purposes.
And, the goals differ by product.

## Page 14

Goals in buying
●
Identity goals: this product helps me to be the person I want to be
●
Hedonic goals: no purpose other than pleasure; candy, jewelry
Implications for advertisers:
What goals are your customers holding when create your ad?
Aligning your ad with one of these goals.
Match = more favorable thoughts.
