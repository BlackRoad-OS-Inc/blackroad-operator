How to Use These Email Templates

Thanks for downloading HubSpot’s Holiday Email Templates! Included in this document are 15 customizable sales email templates to use during the end-of-year holiday season — plus major holidays throughout the year.

We’ve broken down each of these templates by time-of-year for easy discovery.

Once you decide on the template you want to use, simply copy it into your email window and make it your own. You should add, delete, or edit any of the contents in the email as you see fit. We’ve also included [Prompted Language] in brackets for you to add your own information.
