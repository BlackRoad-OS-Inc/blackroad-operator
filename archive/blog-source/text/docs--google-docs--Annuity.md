Okay so I feel like I can make annuities pretty simple for everyone.

You have an IRA. Or a 401k. Some type of qualified funds.  However, if you decide to pass those funds to your children vs. your spouse, that hell, might not even be there. Becomes a huge tax conversation.

Now I’m not saying I know anything but that applies to all qualified funds as of 2019. Post Secure Act.

You can go ahead and use any other vehicle but what if like they literally don’t need it?

Or what if they literally are dead set on not losing any money and having the ability to be in the market?

If you think of annuities as anything short of options, you’re wrong. Now, this can be simple. Or this can be mind boggling.

Previously, income was everywhere. That is not where we are now, and if you are, well, good luck Charlie.

But everything has a trade off when it comes to annuities.

Lets start off kind of with what I am okay with

An annuity that tracks the s&p 500 for 100% downside protection.

Listen, you make a fixed rate in accordance to the performance for a set period of time. This amount is able to compound. A lot quicker since it is credited yearly, has no fees, and the worst that can happen is a 0% credit for the year.
