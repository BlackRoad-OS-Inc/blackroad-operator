Campaign Goal
●
Raise awareness of fashion production and consumption reaching unsustainable levels
●
Speak out on the shared responsibility we have on the environmental impacts of fashion
●
Encourage consumers to shop consciously and look for higher quality and lasting pieces rather than fast fashion
●
Reflect positively on the Levi’s brand in the minds of a younger generation
●
Get people to act on sustainability initiatives and check out Levi’s commitments- water recycling in production process
Target Audience
●
Gen Z! These people are well-known to our generation
●
Get young people to recognize the importance of sustainability in fashion and act 
differently
●
Gen Z is also one of the main contributors to the fast fashion problem
●
Changing the way this audience behaves can change the path of the industry
