### **Guide to Executing Annuity Sales for Ultra 5 Product Using Salesforce and KPIs**

Hello Team,

This guide will walk you through the detailed process of using Salesforce to maximize your outreach, track your success with KPIs, and ensure you're consistently engaging with the right advisors to close more sales for the Ultra 5 annuity product. It’s designed to be comprehensive and step-by-step, so even if you're new to this system or the process, you can follow along easily.

---

### **Step 1: Using the Salesforce Email Templates**

#### **Why This Matters:**

By using Salesforce email templates, we streamline the process of sending personalized communications to advisors. This allows us to focus more on building relationships rather than spending time creating individual emails from scratch.

#### **How to Use the Email Templates:**

1. **Log in to Salesforce** and navigate to your "Tasks" or "Opportunities" tab.

2. **Click on the 'Email' button** (this will open a new email window).

3. **Select the ‘Template’ button** that’s located at the top of the email window.

4. In the template selection window, **search for the relevant template** (e.g., *Ultra 5 Annuity Template* or *Fixed Indexed Annuity Template*).

5. **Select the template** you need, and the body of the email will automatically populate.

6. **Personalize the email** by replacing placeholders like "Dear [Advisor's Name]" with the specific advisor’s name and details.

7. **Double-check the email’s content** to ensure everything is correct before sending.

8. **Click ‘Send’** when you’re ready.

#### **Tip:**

Make sure you're using the **correct template** for each advisor (e.g., don’t send a Fixed Indexed Annuity template to someone interested in Ultra 5).

---

### **Step 2: Tracking KPIs with the HTML Salesforce Report**

#### **Why This Matters:**

KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) help you measure how well your email outreach is working. By tracking open rates, engagement, and click-throughs, you can determine which advisors are most likely to engage with your messages and tailor your outreach accordingly.

#### **How to Track KPIs:**

1. **Access the HTML Tracking Report** in Salesforce:

- Go to the **‘Reports’ tab** in Salesforce.

- Search for the **‘HTML Tracking Report’** and open it.

2. **Analyze Key Metrics:**

- **Open Rates:** Check how many times your emails are being opened by advisors. If an email has a low open rate, you may want to adjust your subject line or follow up with those who haven’t opened it.

- **Click-through Rates:** Look for the number of clicks on links within your emails (e.g., links to the Ultra 5 product page or educational content). This shows you which content advisors find valuable.

- **Resonance with Advisors:** Identify which emails are being opened by certain advisors, and note keywords or themes that seem to resonate most with them. For instance, if “cap rates” tend to get opened more often, focus on emails that emphasize this feature.

3. **Identify Non-Engaging Advisors:**

- **Low Open Rates:** If certain advisors have consistently low open rates, consider removing them from your outreach or following up with a phone call.

- **Historical Trends:** Look at trends in advisor engagement over the last 30-60 days. Advisors who haven’t opened any emails or interacted with any content may be less interested, or it could be time to switch up your approach.

#### **Actionable Insight:**

If you notice that certain advisors repeatedly open your Fixed Indexed Annuity emails but not your Ultra 5 emails, you can re-target them with similar content or personalize your outreach to make it more relevant.

---

### **Step 3: Using the Licensing Email Template for Onboarding Advisors**

#### **Why This Matters:**

The Licensing Email Template helps advisors quickly navigate the appointment process. By using this, you reduce the time spent on back-and-forth communication, allowing advisors to get licensed and selling Ultra 5 faster.

#### **How to Use the Licensing Email Template:**

1. **Go to your Licensing Email Template** in Salesforce (find this under “Email Templates”).

2. **Open the Licensing Template** and **review the content**. The template will have steps and instructions to guide the advisor through the licensing process.

3. **Customize the email** where necessary—make sure the advisor's name and any unique information are added.

4. **Send the email to the advisor** to ensure they complete the required steps for licensing.

#### **Tip:**

Encourage advisors to follow the steps in the email carefully to reduce the time your team spends answering questions and managing the process.

---

### **Step 4: Identifying Advisors to Call Based on Email Engagement**

#### **Why This Matters:**

Knowing who to call is crucial to maximizing your time and resources. By using engagement data from your emails, you can prioritize advisors who are more likely to respond.

#### **How to Identify Advisors to Call:**

1. **Review Your HTML Tracking Report** for any advisors who **opened your emails** multiple times but haven’t responded yet.

2. Focus on **high-engagement advisors** who have opened emails several times (this indicates interest). For example:

- If **Guy Rogers** opened your Fixed Indexed Annuity email 4 times, send him an Ultra 5 email, knowing he’s likely to open and engage.

3. **Check for High-Value Advisors:**

- Look at **production scores** and filter for top producers who haven't received an email in the last 30 days or a call in the last 60 days.

- These are your "Lady Bug" advisors—those who are high producers but might have slipped through the cracks.

- Reach out to these top-tier advisors with personalized emails or calls to reignite interest.

---

### **Step 5: Filtering Advisors by Production Data**

#### **Why This Matters:**

Filtering advisors by production score allows you to prioritize high-performing individuals who are most likely to generate significant business once they’re onboarded.

#### **How to Use the Production Report:**

1. **Access the Production Report** in Salesforce. This will give you an overview of all advisors’ current production numbers and their status.

2. **Filter the list** to show only those advisors who have **high production scores but are not yet appointed**. These are your most valuable leads.

3. **Sort the list from highest to lowest** production scores so you know which advisors to contact first.

4. **Prioritize calls** or emails to the **top 10 producers** who haven’t been contacted in the last 30 days.

#### **Actionable Insight:**

- If you have an advisor like **Hunter Abbe**, who is a top producer but hasn't been contacted in the last 60 days, prioritize reaching out to him. Based on the volume of business he can generate, the potential for conversion is very high.

---

### **Step 6: Handling Undeliverable Emails**

#### **Why This Matters:**

Undeliverable emails can indicate incorrect contact data, which if left unchecked, may lead to missed opportunities.

#### **How to Handle Undeliverable Emails:**

1. **Identify undeliverable emails** in your inbox (these will typically show up with a bounce-back notification).

2. **Review the advisor’s contact details** in Salesforce to ensure the email is correct.

3. **If the email is incorrect**, search for the correct one in Salesforce or contact the advisor to update their details.

4. **Resend the email** after updating the contact info.

5. **Follow up with a call** 15 days after sending the email to confirm receipt and to see if they have any questions or are ready to proceed.

---

### **Step 7: Continuous Optimization & Monthly Review**

#### **Why This Matters:**

A continual review process ensures that your outreach remains effective and responsive to advisor needs. Regular tracking and optimization help refine your strategy.

#### **How to Continuously Optimize Your Outreach:**

1. **Review your outreach efforts** at the end of each month. Which emails got the highest engagement? Which didn’t resonate at all?

2. **Adjust your approach** for the next month based on these insights. For instance:

- If an email with a specific subject line performed well, use that subject line more frequently.

- If certain content (e.g., cap rate information) received higher engagement, incorporate more of that into future emails.

3. **Repeat the process every month**—send new emails, track engagement, make follow-up calls, and adjust accordingly. This cycle ensures that your outreach strategy is always optimized for success.

### **Step 2: Tracking KPIs Using HTML Salesforce Reports**

#### **Why This Matters:**

KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) are critical in understanding the effectiveness of your email campaigns. By tracking these metrics, you can identify which emails are resonating with advisors and who requires further follow-up. This enables you to refine your approach, making your outreach more strategic and effective.

#### **How to Track KPIs in Salesforce:**

1. **Access the HTML Tracking Report:**

- In Salesforce, go to the **‘Reports’ tab** from the main navigation menu.

- In the search bar, type **“HTML Tracking Report”** or find it under your default report templates.

- Open the report to access detailed data on email engagement.

2. **Review Open Rates:**

- **Open rate** shows how many times your email was opened by an advisor. If an email has a high open rate, that means the subject line or the timing of the email was effective. If the open rate is low, you may need to experiment with different subject lines or resend the email at a different time.

- You’ll find this metric listed next to each advisor's name in the report.

**Actionable Tip:**

- If you notice that certain subject lines (e.g., *“Learn About the Ultra 5 Annuity”*) have higher open rates, use similar subject lines in future emails.

3. **Click-through Rates:**

- Click-through rate shows how many recipients clicked on links within the email (for example, links to Ultra 5 product details or appointment scheduling).

- This is an important KPI to track because it indicates the level of engagement beyond just opening the email—whether the advisor found the content valuable enough to explore further.

4. **Engagement by Advisor:**

- Look at the **individual advisor's engagement metrics**. Identify which advisors opened multiple emails or clicked through on links. These are your most engaged prospects and should be prioritized for follow-up.

- For example, if an advisor like **Guy Rogers** opened your *Ultra 5* email 4 times, it indicates strong interest. You should consider following up with him either by email or phone.

5. **Identifying Non-Engaged Advisors:**

- **Low open rates** or no click-throughs are indicators that an advisor might not be engaged. These advisors require additional outreach to reignite their interest.

- You can sort your list to prioritize advisors with high open rates but low click-throughs, suggesting they’re interested but haven’t fully engaged. Reach out to them with a more compelling call to action or a personal phone call.

#### **Actionable Insight:**

- Advisors who frequently open emails but don’t take action (click or respond) could be moved to a **"needs follow-up"** list. You can send them a more personalized follow-up email or call them directly, referencing their past interest.
