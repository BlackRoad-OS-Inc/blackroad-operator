## Slide 7

Summary & Regional Patterns
Top value markets: Romania & Poland in Eastern Europe and Thailand & Singapore in Southeast Asia offer the best mix of affordability and speed (worldpopulationreview.com, worldpopulationreview.com).
Key metrics: Romania ($9/mo, 344/291 Mbps, $0.03 per Mbps); Thailand ($23/mo, 348/284 Mbps, $0.07); Chile ($22/mo, 464/449 Mbps, $0.05); Singapore ($39/mo, 566/498 Mbps, $0.07); Poland ($18/mo, 295/116 Mbps, $0.06).
Regional observations: Eastern Europe and Southeast Asia benefit from competitive markets and fibre networks, yielding low costs and high speeds (worldpopulationreview.com, worldpopulationreview.com), while Sub‑Saharan Africa and Caribbean/Gulf states face average broadband prices over $60 – $80 per month (bestbroadbanddeals.co.uk).
Implication: Policy frameworks that promote competition and fibre deployment reduce cost‑per‑Mbps and unlock digital inclusion.
