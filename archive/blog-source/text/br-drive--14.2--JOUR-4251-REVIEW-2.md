Principles of Compliance

## Page 47

Questions:
Sufﬁciency principle
Tendency to strike a balance between minimizing cognitive effort and 
satisfying motivational concerns. 
Recognition principle
Tendency to go for something we recognize.

## Page 48

Reciprocity
One should be more willing to comply with a request from someone who has 
previously provided a favor or concession

## Page 49

Reciprocity
Door-in-the-face technique

## Page 50

Commitment/consistency
After committing oneself to a position, one should be more willing to comply with 
requests for behaviors that are consistent with that position.
Four walls technique, foot-in-the-door technique, bait-and-switch 
procedure, low-ball technique

## Page 51

Social validation (consensus) 
One should be more willing to comply with a request or behavior if it is consistent 
with what similar others are thinking or doing
Reference group

## Page 52

Friendship/liking
One should be more willing to comply with a request of friends or other liked 
individuals

## Page 54

Scarcity
One should try to secure those opportunities that are scarce or dwindling

## Page 55

Authority
One should be more willing to follow the suggestions of someone who is a 
legitimate authority
