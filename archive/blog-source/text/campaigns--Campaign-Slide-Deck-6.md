CASE STUDY: SECOND HARVEST HEARTLAND
CHALLENGE
Seniors are currently the fastest growing population segment; however, only 25% of 
eligible seniors were taking advantage of SNAP benefits. Second Harvest Heartland 
wanted to generate greater awareness of the SNAP program and drive participation in the 
program. Our objective was to test and learn the best way to reach the audience and 
generate applications. 
STRATEGY
We knew there were specific barriers preventing seniors from tapping into the SNAP 
program. Our team designed a campaign concept to tackle these barriers head on using 
specific program benefits. During our discovery process, we uncovered four key benefits of 
the SNAP program that directly connected with seniors and their caregivers. We used 
these insights to flesh out a messaging test to ultimately gather insights on the audience 
to learn which message resonated the most.
SOLUTION
After learning our audience's motivations, barriers, and interests, we broke down our 
campaign into two parts. We started with awareness messaging to introduce SNAP to 
seniors. Once the awareness phase concluded we launched a lead generation campaign 
that tested different messaging and creative. We continued to optimize the campaigns 
performance by closely monitoring keywords, spend, and cost per conversion. When the 
testing was completed we provided actionable insights on performance by location, 
messaging, platform, and audience segments.

## Page 14

CASE STUDY: SECOND HARVEST HEARTLAND
RESULTS
• Creative focused on budgeting and independence 
resonated the most with our senior and caregiver 
audiences.
• The benefit videos were better at driving conversions 
(survey completions). 
• Although we budgeted more toward Facebook, YouTube 
had greater performance (higher click-throughs to the 
survey) with the senior population than Facebook. 
• Areas with minimal population had the highest click-
through-rates across the board. There is less volume in 
these counties, but there is a great interest for food 
support.
