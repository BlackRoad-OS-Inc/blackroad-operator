Final Takeaways/Recommendations (Include at least 3 points below. Refer to the last two 
paragraphs in this week’s reading for ideas of the types of questions and resulting solutions that 
may arise from your Social Media Audit):
●
Insight 1: Coinbase should expand its Facebook presence to better align with 
competitors’ performance on the site. Otherwise, Coinbase out-competes competitors on 
social media.
●
Insight 2: Putting more Coinbase Earn videos on social media sites could bring more 
traffic to the site and promote the program while still engaging users on social media like 
current content.
●
Insight 3: Twitter should be the primary social media focus because of the account’s 
success. This success comes from keeping posts in line with users’ normal feed (ie. 
trendy, short, funny posts) and posting frequently to stay up to date with current events.
