GET TO KNOW
Minneapolis, MN
What are the best ways to get around in Minneapolis, MN?
This city will please homeowners that appreciate various modes of transportation.
Minneapolis is quite well-suited for those who travel by foot; most daily needs are
convenient to meet without the use of a car. The fairly good public transit infrastructure
allows people to get to many destinations without needing a vehicle as a result of Target
Field Station on the Route 888, and over 150 nearby bus lines. The preferred way to
navigate Minneapolis is very often a car. It is convenient to access a place to park, and it is
a rather short drive to the nearest highway, such as Interstate 35, from anywhere in this
city.
Is Minneapolis, MN pedestrian friendly?
It is straightforward to access the closest supermarket by walking from any location in
Minneapolis. Central Avenue is among the best streets for food stores. In addition,
Minneapolis is a good place in which to go out to eat, and house buyers can enjoy over 300
cafes as well. In terms of education, residents will welcome the proximity to primary as
well as high schools, which are within walking distance from most properties for sale in
Minneapolis.
What is life in Minneapolis, MN like?
People who prefer a relaxed environment will enjoy Minneapolis. Most locations in the city
have fairly good access to green spaces, such as Nokomis Hiawatha Park and Mississippi
Gorge Park, since there are over 150 of them close by for residents to discover, like
Minnehaha Parkway and Lyndale Park. This city is quiet overall, as there isn't a lot of street
noise or city clamour - although there are still several noisier areas, especially around US
Highway 12, Interstate 35, one of the railway lines or the airport (Minneapolis-St Paul
International Airport).

What are the most popular housing types in Minneapolis, MN?
Minneapolis experienced its main construction boom pre-1960, so most of the available
properties are from this time period. In this city, about 45% of dwellings are single
detached homes, while large apartment buildings are the main alternative in the housing
stock. The population residing in this city is evenly split between renters and owners. This
city is primarily composed of one bedroom and two bedroom homes.
