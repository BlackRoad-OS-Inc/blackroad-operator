Subject: What Happens After Your Application is Submitted to Securian

Hi [Advisor's Name],

Once your broker-dealer submits an annuity application to Securian, here’s what happens next:

Application Processing

Once received, our Annuity Services team reviews the application and required forms.

If any forms are not in good order (NIGO), a missing requirements email will be sent to the case manager or financial professional.

Missing requirements can delay contract issuance, so please double-check these common issues:

Licensing & appointment status – Ensure these are completed before the application is signed. Otherwise, new signatures will be required.

Date consistency – The application, replacement form(s), and 1035 Exchange/Transfer/Rollover form must all be signed with the same date.

Annuity Disclosure Statement – Required if your broker-dealer does not perform a suitability review. This form must be completed in full.

Fund Transfers & Processing

If funds were not included with the application, we will request them from the existing carrier once paperwork is in good order.

If funds were included, they will be applied to the contract.

Our Annuity New Business team follows up with the existing carrier regularly until the funds are received.

Follow-Up Timing Based on Transfer Type

Client-Initiated Transfers – We will follow up with you on day 30 and every 30 days thereafter.

Carrier Processing Delays – If the carrier requires specific client action or refuses to provide information, we will follow up as needed.

Check Submissions – If the client submits a check, we will follow up every 30 days after the application is in good order.

Applications are held for a maximum of 6 months if funds are not received.

Contract Issuance & Delivery

Once funds are received and applied, the annuity contract is prepared for delivery.

The contract is mailed based on the broker-dealer’s selling agreement, either to:

The contract holder

The financial professional

The Broker-Dealer/IMO/Agency

After the contract is issued, the contract owner will receive an Account Confirmation separately.

Advisors can view the confirmation online in the Statements and Confirms section of their Book of Business.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out.
