SecureLink Chronic Illness Access

A fixed indexed annuity that allows clients to grow retirement assets while automatically including an Enhanced Surrender Value (ESV) for chronic or terminal illness. If they never need it, the death benefit remains available for their beneficiaries.

At a Glance:

* Available to clients age 75 or younger

* Minimum purchase payment: $20,000 | Maximum: $2 million

* Annual cost for the Roll-up Death Benefit & Enhanced Surrender Value Rider:

* 0.75% of the death benefit value (1.15% if age at issue is 71-75)

How It Works for Clients:

* Need retirement income? Take withdrawals or annuitize the contract.

* Become chronically or terminally ill? Access the Enhanced Surrender Value (after the waiting period) for any expenses.

* Pass away? A guaranteed death benefit is paid to beneficiaries.

Account Options

* Indexed Accounts with 100% downside protection

* Includes S&P 500® 1-Year Point-to-Point with Cap, among others

* Guaranteed Interest Account available

* Transfers allowed within 21 days following each contract anniversary

Death Benefit

The Death Benefit is the greatest of:

* Contract value

* Guaranteed Minimum Surrender Value (GMSV)

* 8% Roll-up Value (purchase payments accumulate at 8% compound interest, up to 200% of contract value)

> Note: Withdrawals reduce the Roll-up Value pro-rata, except RMDs, which are dollar-for-dollar. The Roll-up Value stops increasing at:

>  * Age 85 (oldest owner)

>  * Activation of the Enhanced Surrender Value

>  * Death of the owner (unless continued by a surviving spouse)

>

Enhanced Surrender Value (ESV) for Chronic/Terminal Illness

* Access contract value + any additional amount needed to match the Death Benefit

* No surrender charges or MVA during the Waiver Period

* Qualifying conditions:

* Chronic illness: Inability to perform 2 of 6 ADLs or severe cognitive impairment

* Terminal illness: Life expectancy of 12 months or less

Once activated:

* The Waiver Period begins, and the ESV is available for withdrawal or surrender without penalties

* Any amount exceeding the current contract value moves to the Fixed Account

* The annual charge stops, and the Death Benefit is the greater of contract value or GMSV

Spousal Continuation Options

If a surviving spouse continues the contract, they can choose to:

* Take the Death Benefit immediately (contract value adjusts to Death Benefit value, rider terminates, and annual charge stops)

* Continue the Roll-up Death Benefit & ESV Rider (8% roll-up continues based on spouse’s age, keeping the same benefit triggers)
