SecureLink Ultra 7 is a single payment, fixed indexed annuity. The minimum purchase payment is $20,000, and the maximum is $2 million. You can choose to allocate your purchase payment to any or all of the Indexed Account options available, as well as to the Fixed Account - a guaranteed interest account option. You have the option of either annuitizing (available after the first Contract Anniversary) or surrendering the contract at the earlier of a date that you elect, or by the contract's Maturity Date (age 95).

Index and Crediting Definitions

Index

A statistical composite that measures the ups and downs of the stock, bond and other markets, based on companies in the index. For the SecureLink Ultra 7 annuity, it refers to the underlying index linked to the applicable Indexed Account. One cannot directly invest in an index.

Indexed Account

An account option that may provide interest in the form of an Index Credit at the end of each Crediting Period.

Guaranteed Interest Account(s)

Account options that provide a guaranteed interest rate for a specified period of time.

Fixed Account (1-Year)

A Guaranteed Interest Account that provides an interest rate guaranteed for a one-year time period.

Allocation

Percentage of purchase payments applied to each applicable Indexed Account or Fixed Account option.

Crediting Method

The method by which interest is credited to an Indexed Account.

Interest Applied

For Indexed Accounts, the amount of interest applied is in the form of an Index Credit and applied on Contract Anniversary. For the Fixed Account, interest is credited daily and compounded annually.

Index Credit

The amount of interest credited to values in an Indexed Account at the end of the Crediting Period. The calculation of the Index Credit is determined by the Index Account and Crediting Method. An Index Credit will never be less than zero.

Participation Rate

A percentage representing the portion of the change in the index that will be considered in calculating the Index Credit. The Participation Rate is declared in advance and guaranteed for each Crediting Period.

Cap (Index Cap)

The maximum index growth percentage that will be applied in the calculation of the Index Credit. The Index Cap is declared in advance and guaranteed for each Crediting Period.

Trigger Rate

The rate that will be applied in the calculation of the Index Credit if the performance of the Index meets the trigger condition. The Trigger Rate is declared in advance and guaranteed for each Crediting Period.

Crediting Period

For the Fixed Account, the period of time the fixed interest rate is guaranteed. For Indexed Accounts, the period of time where the rate components of the applicable Crediting Method are guaranteed.

Spread

A percentage that is deducted in calculating the Index Credit. The spread is declared in advance and is guaranteed for the life of the contract.

S&P 500® Index

The S&P 500® Index is a market value-weighted index of 500 publicly- traded companies. S&P identifies important industry groups within the U.S. economy and then allocates a representative sample of stocks within each group to the S&P 500®.

1-Year Point-to-Point with Participation Rate (Crediting Method)

A Point-to-Point Crediting Method measures index growth between two points in time. At the end of the one-year index period, the Index Credit will be determined by comparing the index price at the end of the one- year period with the index price at the beginning of the period. If the change is positive, the percentage of interest credited will be the index return multiplied by the declared Participation Rate.

1-Year Point-to-Point with Cap (Crediting Method)

A Point-to-Point Crediting Method measures index growth between two points in time. At the end of the one-year index period, the Index Credit will be determined by comparing the index price at the end of the one- year period with the index price at the beginning of the period. If the change is positive, the amount of interest will be credited in the same percentage as the index return, up to the declared Index Cap.

1-Year Point-to-Point with Term Guarantee Cap (Crediting Method)

A Point-to-Point with Term Guarantee Crediting Method measures index growth between two points in time and is only available for allocation at contract issues. At the end of the one-year index period, the Index Credit will be determined by comparing the index price at the end of the one- year period with the index price at the beginning of the period. If the change is positive, the amount of interest will be credited in the same percentage as the index return, up to the declared Index Cap. The cap rate is guaranteed for the duration of the 7 year surrender charge period.

1-Year Performance Trigger (Crediting Method)

A Performance Trigger Crediting Method compares index values at two points in time. At the end of the one-year index period, the Index Credit will be determined by comparing the index price at the end of the one- year period with the index price at the beginning of the period. If the change is positive or zero, the amount of interest will be credited in the same percentage as the declared Trigger Rate.

Initial Rate

Initial Rate as used in this illustration may apply to Participation Rate, Trigger Rate, Cap or Interest Rate. Initial Rates are set at contract issue and guaranteed for the applicable Crediting Period.

Guaranteed Rate

Guaranteed Rate as used in this illustration may apply to Participation Rate, Trigger Rate, Cap or Interest Rate. Guaranteed Rates are set at contract issue and represent the minimum rate that may be applied during the life of the contract.

Guaranteed Minimum Surrender Value Rates

Guaranteed Minimum Surrender Value Rates are set at contract issue and guaranteed for the life of the contract. Rates are used to determine the minimum amount available upon surrender, death or annuitization.

nual Effective Rate

The equivalent annual rate earned over the time period shown that would produce the resulting Contract Value.

Index Return

The percentage change in an index over the course of a particular crediting period. Index returns can be positive or negative.

Rate Applied

The interest rate credited after applying any applicable Participation Rate, Trigger Rate or Cap.

Aggregate Credited Rate

The percentage change in Contract Value after applying any Index Credits and/or interest where applicable.

Annuity Product Definitions

Contract Anniversary/Age

Contract Anniversary represents the last day of the first contract year and for each succeeding year. The age shown represents your hypothetical age on the last day of each contract year shown.

Purchase Payment

Hypothetical amount contributed to the annuity.

Withdrawal

Hypothetical amount taken out of the annuity. The minimum withdrawal is $250. Withdrawal amounts shown are prior to the deduction of any applicable Surrender Charge or positive/negative MVA. Additionally, withdrawals illustrated do not reflect the effect of income taxes, penalty taxes and/or premium taxes (where applicable).

Guaranteed Minimum Surrender Value (GMSV)

The minimum amount available to you upon surrender, death, or annuitization of the contract. The GMSV equals a percentage of purchase payments accumulated at a guaranteed rate of interest (1-3%), adjusted for withdrawals.

Contract Value

The sum of your values in the Fixed Account and Indexed Accounts on any valuation date prior to annuitization.

Surrender Value

The amount that would generally be payable to you on surrender of this contract. The Surrender Value is equal to the greater of the Guaranteed Minimum Surrender Value or the Contract Value less Surrender Charges, and when applicable, any applicable MVA.

Death Benefit

The Death Benefit is the amount payable to your Beneficiaries upon your death. The Death Benefit is equal to the greater of the Contract Value or the Guaranteed Minimum Surrender Value.

Spousal Continuance

At death, a spouse sole beneficiary or spouse joint owner can elect to keep the annuity contract in place. Death benefit guarantees continue based on the age of the surviving spouse (where applicable).

Annuitization

Annuitization is the process of converting your Contract Value into a regular stream of Annuity Income Payments. You can convert your annuity into a fixed income stream (via Annuitization) under the base provisions of your contract at any time after the first Contract Anniversary up through the contract Maturity Date. A variety of Annuity Income Options are available. Your income is determined by the Annuity Income Option you select at the then current Annuity Income Rates.

For purposes of this illustration, once the contract is annuitized all values for years following annuitization would be zero. Once annuitized, amounts payable to beneficiaries upon death would be determined based on the terms of the annuity income option selected. All other contract features are terminated.
