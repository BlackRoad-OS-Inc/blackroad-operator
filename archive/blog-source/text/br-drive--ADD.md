ADHD / Focus & Executive Function Concerns

Patient: Alexa Louise Amundson

Date: [insert date]

1. Reason for Visit

I would like to be evaluated for ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder) or related conditions.

I’ve noticed long-standing patterns in focus, organization, time management, and follow-through that are significantly affecting my daily life, work, and mental health. These patterns seem consistent with adult ADHD, and I’m asking for a professional evaluation and guidance.

2. How My Brain Works Day-to-Day (Symptoms as I Experience Them)

A. Attention and Focus

I struggle to stay focused on one task, even when it’s important or meaningful to me.

I frequently start many projects or tasks and have difficulty finishing them.

I get pulled into side-tracks easily (new ideas, thoughts, tools, tabs, etc.).

My mind often feels like it has “too many tabs open” at once, and it’s hard to keep a single line of thought.

B. Hyperfocus

I can go into deep hyperfocus on certain things (e.g., building systems, coding, designing, math, AI/agent work, planning my OS/infra) for hours at a time.

When I’m in this state, I often:

Lose track of time

Forget basic needs (eating, breaks, sleep, messages)

I have trouble pulling myself out of hyperfocus once I’m in it.

C. Organization & Follow-Through

I generate complex, detailed ideas and systems, but:

Keeping them organized is hard (files, repos, tools, notes, etc. become chaotic).

Finishing the “boring” or repetitive parts (cleanup, documentation, admin steps) is very difficult.

I often leave things half-done and jump to the next idea or project.

D. Time Management & Prioritization

I frequently underestimate how long tasks will take.

Time passes without me realizing it (“where did the last 3 hours go?”).

I have trouble:

Deciding what to do first when there are multiple important tasks.

Sticking to a plan instead of being pulled by whatever grabs my attention in the moment.

E. Mental Load / Restlessness

My thoughts move quickly and jump between topics.

It can be exhausting trying to hold everything in my head at once.

I often feel mentally “on” all the time and have difficulty slowing down or fully relaxing.

3. How This Is Impacting My Life

A. Work / Projects / Goals

I am capable of high-level, complex thinking and have big, clear visions for my projects and systems.

However, my difficulty with focus, organization, and follow-through means:

Many projects stay unfinished or stuck in partial states.

Important tasks get delayed or lost among all the other ideas.

It’s hard to convert my ideas into consistent, stable output.

B. Day-to-Day Functioning

Tasks pile up and become overwhelming because I don’t close loops well (emails, cleanup, maintenance, etc.).

I can end up neglecting important responsibilities while deep in a hyperfocus tunnel on something else.

This creates stress, self-blame, and a sense that I’m “underperforming” compared to what I know I’m capable of.

C. Emotional / Mental Health Impact

I feel frustrated that my brain can do complex things but struggles with basics like organization, consistency, and time.

I experience stress and anxiety around unfinished work and chaotic systems.

It feels like my abilities and my actual execution don’t match, and that gap is painful.

4. History / Duration

These patterns are not new.

I have noticed similar traits for years:

Being distractible

Having many projects and ideas going at once

Struggling with time management, follow-through, and organization

As my responsibilities have grown, the impact has become more obvious and more disruptive.

5. What I Am Asking For

A full evaluation for ADHD in adults, including:

Assessment of my symptoms and history

Ruling out or considering other possible explanations (e.g., anxiety, depression, sleep issues, etc.)

Help understanding:

Whether ADHD (inattentive, hyperactive/impulsive, or combined type) is a good explanation for what I’m experiencing

If not, what else might better fit my patterns.

Guidance on treatment and support options, which may include:

Medication (if appropriate)

Therapy or coaching (especially around executive function)

Practical strategies for managing focus, time, and organization

Possible things to talk through together:

Specific examples from my recent life where ADHD-like symptoms caused problems

Any relevant medical, family, or mental health history

How to build a plan that supports both my high-capacity/creative side and my need for structure
