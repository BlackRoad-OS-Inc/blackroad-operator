Certainly, here's a step-by-step guide on organizing and using your Google Drive folder effectively:

Learn How to Use Access Organized through our Google Drive Folder

Step 1: College Class Folder

Start by accessing your Google Drive folder.

Locate the "College Class Folder," which serves as the foundation for your collaborative work.

Step 2: Class Breakdowns

Inside the "College Class Folder," you'll find a breakdown of individual classes, each relevant to filling out the templates.

Within each class folder, you will discover the following resources:

**Relevant Readings**: Store all relevant reading materials for the class.

**Class Lectures**: Keep class lectures that outline important steps for accumulating and maintaining target ideas.

**Additional Assignments**: Include any additional submitted assignments to aid in template execution.

Step 3: Important Updates

Among these organized folders, you'll also find a document named "Important Updates."

This document is not categorized into folders and is meant for all contributors to use.

Use "Important Updates" to track execution progress and share essential information throughout your collaboration.

Step 4: Most Important Helps

Discover a document titled "Most Important Helps" in the main folder.

This document is designed to trigger brainstorming sessions and boost your template filling process.

Step 5: Pemberton Homes Media Information

Explore another folder containing "Pemberton Homes Media Information."

This resource can provide valuable insights and information related to Pemberton Homes.

Step 6: School Folders Priority

Before filling out any templates, prioritize reviewing your school folders.

After completing each template to the best of your ability, follow these steps:

Make a copy of the document.

Move the copy to the "Submit for Opinion" folder.

Within "Submit for Opinion," locate the "Submission" folder.

Await review, and once reviewed, move the submission to the "Reviewed" folder.

Collaborate back-and-forth as needed.

The final, refined template will be placed into the "Final Template" folder.

By following these steps, you can efficiently organize, collaborate, and manage your Google Drive folder to streamline your workflow and enhance productivity.
