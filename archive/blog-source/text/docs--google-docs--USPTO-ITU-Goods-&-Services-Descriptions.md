Trademark Intent-to-Use (ITU) Application Descriptions

For BlackRoad Inc.'s Layered Strategy

This document provides recommended, specific language for the Goods and Services identifications for the new Intent-to-Use (ITU) trademark applications. Using acceptable descriptions from the USPTO Manual of Acceptable Identifications is critical for minimizing office actions.

1. BLACKSTREAM (Primary Class 36)

Purpose: To cover the financial advisory and management arm, explicitly distinguishing it from BlackRock's core institutional investment management (Class 36) and Black Stream Capital's real estate niche (also Class 36).

2. ROADCHAIN (Classes 9 and 42)

Purpose: To cover the underlying decentralized technology and infrastructure software layer.

3. ROADCOIN (Classes 9 and 42)

Purpose: To cover the network token/transactional element of the infrastructure.
