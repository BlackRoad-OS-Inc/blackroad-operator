BlackStream BD & RIA Formation and Licensing Strategy

Establishing a new Broker-Dealer (BD) and a Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) firm, collectively under the BlackStream entity, are separate, highly regulated processes overseen by different bodies (FINRA/SEC/State Regulators).

This is a general guide; consult with a specialized compliance attorney or consultant (like a 'compliance counsel' or 'registration specialist') to handle the detailed filing process.

1. Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) Formation

The RIA registration determines who you advise and how much you manage (Assets Under Management or AUM).

A. Initial Steps & Registration Type

Select Legal Entity: Form the legal entity for BlackStream (e.g., BlackStream, LLC or BlackStream, Inc.).

Determine Jurisdiction:

SEC Registration (Federal): Required if BlackStream will manage over $100 Million in AUM.

State Registration: Required if BlackStream manages under $100 Million AUM, typically registering in the state of the principal office (Minnesota, in your case) and any states where you have clients.

Form ADV Filing (CRITICAL): The main document for RIA registration. This is filed through the Investment Adviser Registration Depository (IARD) system.

Part 1A/1B: Details about the firm, ownership, disciplinary history (yours will be cross-referenced with your CRD number: 7794541), and employees.

Part 2A (Brochure): The primary disclosure document given to clients, covering services, fees, conflicts of interest, and business practices.

Part 2B (Brochure Supplements): Disclosure documents for the individuals who advise clients.

B. Licensing and Personnel

Principal/Advisory Licenses: The founder/principal must hold appropriate licenses:

Series 65 (Uniform Investment Adviser Law Examination), OR

Series 66 (Uniform Combined State Law Examination) AND a qualifying principal's license (like a Series 7/6/etc.).

Transferring Licenses: Your personal licenses (Series 7/66/etc. based on your FINRA BrokerCheck file) are portable. Once BlackStream is registered (either with the SEC or state), you will file a Form U4 (Uniform Application for Securities Industry Registration or Transfer) to associate your existing licenses with the new firm.

Note: Your existing licenses are currently inactive since you are not associated with a registered firm. They become active again once BlackStream is fully registered and you are associated with it.

2. Broker-Dealer (BD) Formation

The BD registration is required if BlackStream intends to engage in the business of buying and selling securities for its own account or for others, or if it charges a commission.

A. Initial Steps and FINRA Membership

Form BD Filing: File the Form BD through the Central Registration Depository (CRD) system (the same system used for your personal CRD# 7794541).

FINRA New Member Application (NMA): This is a rigorous and lengthy application process required to become a FINRA member firm. This includes:

Detailed business plan and financial projections.

Demonstration of adequate net capital.

Identification of all supervisors and personnel.

Clear organizational structure and detailed written supervisory procedures (WSPs).

Mandatory Licensing: BlackStream must hire or designate individuals who hold the necessary FINRA principal licenses to supervise the firm:

Series 24 (General Securities Principal): Required to manage and supervise the general securities activities of the firm.

Series 79 (Investment Banking Representative) or Series 82 (Private Securities Offerings Representative) may be required depending on the specific services offered (e.g., venture capital or private placement offerings).

B. Key Infrastructure Requirements

Net Capital: The firm must maintain a minimum level of liquid capital, which varies depending on the type of business.

Supervisory System: The firm needs detailed Written Supervisory Procedures (WSPs), compliance manuals, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) programs, and an internal audit program.

Registered Office: FINRA must approve the physical location of the office where securities activities are conducted.
