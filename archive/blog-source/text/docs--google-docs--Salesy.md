Learned this phone strategy years ago, and it works 90% of the time… Once the receptionist answers the phone, state your full name, and that you’re calling for X.

Ex:  “Good morning, Eby Construction, this is Mary, how may i direct your call?" “Paul Kranopolis calling for Jim Winter.” You've already taken their most used follow up question off the table (who's calling?), so they're probably a little off script, as you've changed the progression of what 90% of the calls they normally receive.  So there is a high likelihood you get by from there.  If they do ask a follow up question such as: "what's this regarding?", your answer is "it's a personal business matter".  99.9% likely to get past the GK with maximum of 2 interactions.

Your tone should be measured and confident.  Assume you're going to get thru because you are.
