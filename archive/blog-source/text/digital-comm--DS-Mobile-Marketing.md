## Slide 14

Mobile Marketing

Definition: a multi-channel, digital marketing strategy aimed at reaching a target audience on their smartphones, tablets, and/or other mobile devices, via websites, email, SMS and MMS, social media, and apps.

Effective mobile advertising means understanding your mobile audience, designing content with mobile platforms in mind, and making strategic use of SMS/MMS marketing and mobile apps.

Not a standalone effort but rather supports your other digital strategies.


## Slide 15

Steps in your mobile strategy

1) Create mobile buyer personas.

2) Set your goals.

3) Establish KPIs.

4) Monitor mobile metrics.


## Slide 16

Building a mobile buyer persona

Depending a person’s mobile device type, internet speed/connectivity and comfort level with technology there are certain tasks people will and won’t do on mobile.

Understand how much of their web usage happens on mobile devices? Are they comfortable completing a purchase on a smartphone?

Mobile fun facts:

65% of all email is first opened on a mobile device.

48% of users start their mobile internet sessions on a search engine.

56% of B2B buyers frequently use smartphones to access vendors’ content.

95% of adults primarily use their smartphones to access content/information.


## Slide 17

Set your goals.

Start by establishing what you’re currently doing on mobile and how those initiatives are performing?

Determine what are your main objectives for including mobile marketing in your overall strategy?

For example: increase conversions from email marketing or drive traffic to product pages.

Understand how are you engaging your mobile audience cross-channel? This will help you establish all the key touch points on the mobile customer journey.


## Slide 18

Establish KPIs to define your mobile campaign’s success.

3 primary KPIs: Engagement, acquisition and customer service.

Engagement—Provide mobile-friendly content for potential customers who are searching for information about your industry or product. Make sure your website is mobile-responsive to improve mobile SEO.

Acquisition—Make sure lead nurturing emails are mobile-friendly with clear calls-to-action. Buttons in emails should be near the top of the message and be big enough to easily tap in order to facilitate click-throughs. Then make it as easy as possible for someone to fill out a form on your mobile-optimized landing page.

Customer Service— Allow your customers to easily reach you through any platform they want, including simple click-to-call buttons.


## Slide 19

Monitor mobile metrics.

Mobile behavior data reveals how well your mobile content engages your audience.

Mobile conversion data will indicate whether or not some of your key landing pages still need to be optimized for mobile browsing.


## Slide 20

Mobile optimized website best practices

All websites must be optimized for mobile devices to rank high in SEO.

The mobile user experience directly correlates to the sales cycle and how often someone converts.

Considerations:

Content fits on the screen without side-to-side scrolling or zooming.

Content loads quickly.

Site returns no mobile-specific errors.


## Slide 21

Mobile optimized email best practices.

Optimize for 38 character or less mobile subject lines.

Creating a unique landing page for an email marketing campaign will ensure only the most relevant information is displayed.

Remember that readers are using their fingers to select items. Use pronounced image buttons and keep the layout simple.

Keep forms minimal. The fewer fields, the better.

Most email marketing providers will use responsive design—a strategy that automatically formats web page content for optimal viewing on any device—but there are still some key considerations for designing email CTAs with mobile users in mind:

Place the CTA early in the message (above the fold whenever possible).

Make buttons at least 44x44 pixels, so they are easily “tap-able.”


## Slide 22

SMS (Short Messaging Service) aka Texting Best Practices

The open rate of SMS is 98% compared to 22% for emails.

Text messages can be 8x more effective at engaging customers.

Best practices:

Keep the text under 160 characters.

Don’t use slang or abbreviations.

Offer the recipient something of value.

Make it clear who is sending the message.

Craft a clear call-to-action.

Best legal practices under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA):

Adequate notice: let consumers know that they’ll receive SMS messages

Offer clear opt-in and opt-out options


## Slide 23

Other Mobile Tools

MMS (multimedia message service) can include text, photos, videos, audios, or GIFs. Expanded media options allow for a more branded message and create a better tie-in to other marketing campaigns.

Apps and push notifications:

To maximize an app’s impact be involved in the entire process, from app development through implementation.

In-app and push notifications could include:

Reminders

Promotional messages

Calls-to-action for specific events or goals

Messages that are highly personalized based on user profiles

In-app and push notifications can deepen engagements.

Introduce new app features to your users.

Send messages to promote engagement with specific content pieces.

Drive conversions by delivering targeted CTAs at specific levels of engagement.
