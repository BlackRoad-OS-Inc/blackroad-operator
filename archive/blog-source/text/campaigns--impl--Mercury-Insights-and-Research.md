SECONDARY
RESEARCH
01
Within the Latin American and
Caribbean region, the incorporation of
electric vehicles in the Caribbean is
happening to a greater degree. One
example is Cayo Largo, Cuba, which
aims to become an eco-friendly tourist
destination using electric vehicles
purchased from Cayman Automotive.
Other Caribbean islands increasing
usage of electric vehicles include
Barbados and Trinidad, thanks to lower
import duties to encourage imports. For
greater impact over the longterm, Latin
America and Caribbean governments
will need to take an active role in
incentivising this migration to
alternative fuels.
ARTICLE 1
02
Consumers expect brands to reduce
their damage to the environment, as
examined by the Sustainability Pillar of
the Surroundings Driver, and eventually
all vehicles will be either electric, solar-
powered or fuelled by another
renewable resource.
ARTICLE 2
03
With consumers changing their
lifestyles to adopt more sustainable
habits to protect the planet, they are
increasingly demanding brands do the
same. As a result brands are innovating
to create new, more environmentally
friendly systems to save resources,
reduce waste or eliminate carbon
emissions. We have seen attempts to
tackle significant emissions created by
the shipping industry with smart sails to
utilise wind power in a modern way,
using biofuel, and even robots to keep
hulls clean to reduce drag and therefore
fuel consumption.
