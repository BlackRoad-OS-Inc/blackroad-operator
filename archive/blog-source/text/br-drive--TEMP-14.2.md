Principles of Compliance

Questions:

Sufficiency principle

Tendency to strike a balance between minimizing cognitive effort and  satisfying motivational concerns.

Recognition principle

Tendency to go for something we recognize.

Reciprocity

One should be more willing to comply with a request from someone who has  previously provided a favor or concession

Reciprocity

Door-in-the-face technique

Commitment/consistency

After committing oneself to a position, one should be more willing to comply with  requests for behaviors that are consistent with that position.

Four walls technique, foot-in-the-door technique, bait-and-switch  procedure, low-ball technique

Social validation (consensus)

One should be more willing to comply with a request or behavior if it is consistent  with what similar others are thinking or doing

Reference group

Friendship/liking

One should be more willing to comply with a request of friends or other liked  individuals

Scarcity

One should try to secure those opportunities that are scarce or dwindling

Authority

One should be more willing to follow the suggestions of someone who is a  legitimate authority
