Where do I even start? Do I give the condensed version to you guys? I guess I can start by saying, this is my dream. My dream is working with people everyday, all day. Some days, this was the only thing I knew for sure.

I remember starting college wondering where the long road ahead would take me. Would I end up in a cubicle? Would I end up moving across the U.S. to Silicon Valley to be a “keynote speaker” for a to die for tech company? Oh no, then I ended up deciding, “Oh, I get a little jittery in front of big audiences.” So, obviously that cool little dream had to be put to rest. Next, came the, “oh, yes, vp of sales at a Fortune 500 company.” I realized then, did I really want a boss? Did I really need one? Can’t forget the time I thought I was going to be a graphic designer, an architect, an interior designer, etc.

I really didn’t expect anything to fall into place or nearly that quick.
