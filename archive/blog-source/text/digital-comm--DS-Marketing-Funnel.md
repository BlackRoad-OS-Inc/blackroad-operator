## Slide 6

Interest and Consideration

I know Crumbl Cookies exists but am I intrigued enough to learn more and consider them? What makes them better than others (prices, service, product, location/convenience?)

Interest: I’m learning more, I go to their website and look at their products. Maybe I’m in, maybe I’m out.

Consideration: I’m actively considering them; I might compare their prices to other companies. I’ll look at Google or other sites reviews to see how others feel about the product.


## Slide 7

Conversion: Intent, Evaluation, Purchase

Intent: I’ve given you a very strong signal that I’m interested. Maybe I put things into my Crumbl Cookie shopping cart but didn’t check out.

Evaluation: I make the final decision that I’m going to buy a product or submit an order.

Purchase: When a transaction takes place. You’ve gone from a prospect to a customer. I bought the cookies, I’ve booked a flight.

This is the phase that your senior leaders will care about the most. It may be your job to tell them that you need to get prospects through the other phases before you get to here.


## Slide 8

B2B vs. B2C

B2C is smaller: I may be an individual looking to buy a new pair of shoes. I may not do any research but go straight to shopping (intent) because of a strong recommendation from a family or friend.

B2B: Small businesses may be an individual. Large corporations, the buying process can start with an RFP (Request for Proposal) where they solicit proposals for various suppliers. They may work directly with a single point of contact salesperson.

## Slide 12

The Customer Experience Funnel

Repeat: Continue bottom of the funnel activities to encourage me to become a repeat customer. Make me want to buy Crumbl Cookies the next time I need a thank you gift.

Loyalty: Make me prefer Crumbl Cookies over all others. We have a lot of Target loyalty in this class and in MN. Engagement matters, make me feel good about preferring this brand.

What brands are you loyal to?

Referral/Advocacy: You actively refer this brand to others, could be word of mouth, social media posts, leaving a review of your great experience. Social media influencers do a lot of this.
