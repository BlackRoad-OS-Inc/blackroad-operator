## Page 22

Depth of processing
It is not just about repetition. It is also about the level of processing.
Remembering comes from processing deeply, rather than superﬁcially.
Thus, depending on what you (are asked to) do with the information can 
affect how deeply you process it.
RIVER: Is the word in capital letters?
HOUSE: Does the word rhyme with mouse?
WINE: Does the word fit in the sentence: “The man drank a glass of….”
