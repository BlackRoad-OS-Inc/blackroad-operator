Grids:

Most Popular: we create lines, copy and rotate them

Other: we create a grid with a few clicks using the grid tool, where all lines are already created

Create a new document, click web, enter 800 for the width, and 800 for the height, click create

For grids using lines: select fill and click on none, then zoom out a little bit, go to line segment tool,

Draw a line an inch off of the white square off to the left, hold shift while doing so, then press v for selection tool, hold out, start to move the line to the right, and hold shift also.. You should have alt + shift. Make a copy. Then hold CTRL D to make a lot of copies. Select all of the lines and go to properties panel . In stroke, select “all 25 points.” (0.25). Then change opacity to 40%. Close the properties panel and hit ctrl g to group these lines. After this select rotate tool, and simply press enter. You should rotate to 60 degrees, click on copy. After this we will create another copy, and flip it along the horizontal axis… so press ctrl C ctrl F, go to properties panel, and flip it along the left to right triangle option. Select all the layers, in align panel, click more options, align to art board, now click horizontal align center, and on vertical align center. We must scale if there are white triangles in the corner by simply zooming out and holding. Draw from top left corner to bottom right and then press V for selection tool, again select all layers but right click, and select make clipping mask. Now you’re ready to work!

2nd way!!!!

Select grid tool, then rectangular grid tool, press enter, enter 300 for the horizontal and vertical dividers, click ok. Draw a rectangle. Go to properties, and switch stroke to .25 and the click opacity to 40%. After this go to scale tool, double click scale tool, in vertical scale enter the value 86.602, click ok. Then click shear tool, press enter, and then enter 30 degrees and then click ok. lastly click rotate tool click enter, and rotate to -30 degrees, then click ok. We need to scale then, so zoom out and drag so the white triangles arent there. Deselect the grid, press M for rectangle tool. Draw a rectangle from corner to corner. Press V for selection tool, select all the layers, right click, click right clipping mask
