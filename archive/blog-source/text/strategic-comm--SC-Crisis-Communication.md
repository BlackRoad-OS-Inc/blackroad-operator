## Slide 7

PHASE 1 –

INSTRUCTING INFORMATION!!!!!!

KEEP PEOPLE SAFE

Protect people from physical harm – 9/11 – walk north! Stay calm!

Be honest about risks – 9/11 would have liked folks to not be north of Canal Street 	either, but…. Could not clear the city fast enough (no transit running). So, prioritized 	risks!

ADJUSTING INFORMATION

Deal psychologically with the crisis.

What happened, how it is being prevented.

## Slide 10

PHASE 2 –

REPUTATION MANAGEMENT

verbal (what you say) and nonverbal (what you do)

You can take action without claiming responsibility – we care about our customers! Tylenol did this. It was some madman who did it, but we will take our products off the shelf and make them even safer.

YOU should understand TABLE 8.1/8.2 in the Coombs reading for the exam!!!!

Postures – denial, diminish, rebuilding and bolstering, know the techniques for each, and know the consequences

Crisis – Content of message


## Slide 11

PHASE 2 –

Denial – attacking accuser (rumor), denying the crisis (rumor), scapegoating (avoid this)

Diminish – excusing (not our intention, use when low level of responsibility), justification (victims deserved it, use when low level of respon.)

Rebuilding – compensation (visible victims), apology (when you are the primary one responsible)

Bolstering - Reminding of past good works (when its

true), ingratiating (praise stakeholders, when they help), victimage 	(organization is also a victim, hacking, product tampering)
