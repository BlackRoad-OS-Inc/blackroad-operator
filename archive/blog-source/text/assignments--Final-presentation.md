Social Media Audit Insights
Twitter should be the primary social media focus because of the account’s success.
This success comes from keeping posts in line with users’ normal feed (ie. trendy,
short, funny posts) and posting frequently to stay up to date with current events.
Putting more Coinbase Earn videos on social media sites could bring more
traffic to the site and promote the program while still engaging users on
social media like current content.
