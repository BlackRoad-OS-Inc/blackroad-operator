The SECURE Act of 2019 changed the rules governing distributions from qualified retirement plans and IRAs. The “Stretch IRA” is now unavailable for most beneficiaries. The new section on the SECURE Act will discuss the new changes. However, not all of the changes are completely understood because questions remain. Until further guidance is provided, questions will remain.

And, in terms of the tax provisions applying to them, the two types are: (1) “qualified” and (2) “non-qualified”. Further, we can say that there are two types, in terms of how premiums are paid”. Thus, (1) “single premium” and (2) “flexible premium”. But when we classify them in terms of when income payments will begin, we need three classes: (1) “immediate”, (2) “deferred”, and (3) “deferred income” or “longevity”.

This is because the various types of annuities we’ve just noted are very different because they are designed to do very different jobs.
