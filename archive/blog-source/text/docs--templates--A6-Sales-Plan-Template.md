Goal #1

[Explain the first goal and copy/paste this section as needed for more tactics in your action plan.]

[Establish Brand Recognition and Trust]

Objective 1:

Increase website traffic by 20% within the first six months by optimizing content for relevant keywords and providing valuable resources.

Objective 2:

Achieve a 15% increase in social media engagement (likes, shares, comments) over the course of one year through consistent and engaging content sharing.

Objective 3:

Gain 10 positive online reviews and testimonials from satisfied older clients within the first year to build trust and credibility.

Goal #2

[Educate and Empower Older Clients]

Objective 1:

Grow the email subscriber list by 15% in the first six months by offering valuable resources and incentives for sign-up.

Objective 2:

Host a minimum of four webinars or live streams within the first year, with an average attendance rate of at least 50 participants per session, to educate older clients on real estate topics relevant to their needs.

Objective 3:

Publish a minimum of two blog posts or articles per month addressing the unique needs and interests of older individuals in the real estate market.
