FAQ

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards through Stripe. No crypto at this time.
Is there a free trial for paid plans?
The Free plan has no time limit. Paid plans include a 14-day trial with full features.
Where is data stored?
Data runs on our sovereign infrastructure. No third-party cloud providers hold your data.
Can I self-host?
Studio plan includes self-hosting documentation and deployment scripts for your own hardware.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. Full refund within 30 days if the product is not right for you. No questions asked.
