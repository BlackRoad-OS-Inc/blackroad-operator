Our Principles

Video should serve you.

01

Attention is sacred

We will never design features that exploit attention. No infinite scroll. No variable reward schedules. No dark patterns.

02

Creators own their work

Every video remains the creator's property. No platform license. No content ID disputes. Upload it, own it, remove it anytime.

03

Quality over quantity

We do not optimize for upload volume. One excellent video matters more than a hundred filler posts. Our agents recommend depth.

04

No ads, no sponsors, no compromise

Funded by BlackRoad OS subscriptions and direct creator support through RoadPay. The viewer is the customer, not the product.
