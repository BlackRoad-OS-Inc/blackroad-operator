Safety Layer

Defense in depth for young minds

Every feature in CarSeat was designed with child safety experts. Not as an afterthought. Not as a toggle buried in settings. Safety is the architecture.

FLT

AI Content Filter

Local AI scans all content before it reaches your child's screen. Not keyword matching -- semantic understanding. Catches subtle inappropriate content that keyword filters miss. Runs entirely on-device so nothing is sent to any cloud.

On-device / semantic / zero-cloud
CLK

Screen Time Controls

Set daily time limits per app, per category, or total. Gentle wind-down warnings before time runs out. Bedtime mode dims screens and limits to approved apps. Weekend schedules separate from weekday. Parents adjust from their own device.

Per-app / scheduled / remote control
LOC

Location Awareness

Know where your children are without invasive tracking. Geofence alerts when they arrive at or leave school, home, or custom locations. Location sharing is opt-in and visible to the child -- no secret surveillance.

Geofence / consent-based / transparent
COM

Communication Controls

Approve contacts before your child can message them. AI monitors conversations for bullying, grooming, or predatory patterns -- without reading message content (pattern detection, not content surveillance).

Contact whitelist / pattern detection
EDU

Learning Mode

AI tutor that helps with homework, explains concepts, and encourages curiosity. Adapts to your child's grade level and learning style. Never gives direct answers -- guides the child to discover the answer themselves.

Adaptive / Socratic method / age-aware
PNL

Parent Dashboard

See app usage, screen time trends, content filter reports, and activity summaries. Weekly digest emails. Adjust settings from any device. No complex menus -- the dashboard shows what matters and lets you act on it.

Real-time / weekly digest / remote
Age-Appropriate AI

Different ages. Different needs.

Ages 3-5

Sandbox

Curated educational apps only. No text input. Voice-guided learning games. Colors, shapes, letters, numbers. Maximum 30 minutes/day default. Every app parent-approved before first launch.

Ages 6-9

Explorer

Expanded app library. AI reading companion. Math tutor. Science explorer. Supervised web browsing with allowlist. Message parents only. 1-hour default screen time. Creative tools for drawing and music.

Ages 10-12

Builder

Broader web access with content filter. Messaging with approved contacts. Introduction to coding through visual programming. AI writing assistant with age-appropriate guidance. 2-hour default screen time.

Ages 13+

Navigator

Full web access with safety net. Social features with monitoring. Full BlackRoad OS tools with guardrails. AI reasoning tools. Gradual independence with parent visibility. Customizable time limits.

Our Promise

The CarSeat Privacy Pledge

Your child is not a data point. Their attention is not inventory. Their behavior is not a product to be sold. CarSeat exists to protect children in the digital world, not to profit from them.
+

Zero data collection on children

CarSeat does not collect, store, or transmit any data about your child to any server. All AI processing happens locally on your device. No behavioral profiles. No usage analytics. Nothing.

+

Zero advertising to children

CarSeat will never show ads to children. Not now. Not ever. There is no business model that involves monetizing children's attention. The product is the product.

+

Zero manipulative design

No infinite scroll. No autoplay. No streak mechanics. No notification bombardment. No dark patterns. CarSeat is designed to be put down. Technology should serve children, not addict them.

+

COPPA and beyond

Full compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and every applicable child privacy regulation. But compliance is the floor, not the ceiling. CarSeat exceeds every legal requirement because the law is always behind the technology.
