What Is RoadCoin

A compute accounting unit

RoadCoin is the credit system for the BlackRoad mesh network. It tracks compute contributed and consumed across every node.

Not a cryptocurrency

RoadCoin is not a blockchain token, not a speculative asset, and not traded on exchanges. It is a deterministic accounting unit that tracks compute cycles across the BlackRoad mesh. One RoadCoin represents a fixed unit of inference compute -- measurable, auditable, and tied to real hardware output.

No mining. No proof-of-work. No gas fees. Just math tied to silicon.

Self-hosted by design

Credits are issued when your hardware performs real work. They are consumed when you request inference from the mesh. The ledger lives on RoadChain -- a witnessing ledger that records every compute event with cryptographic proof. Your node, your credits, your ownership.

Every credit traces back to a real inference event on real hardware.
How It Works

Earn by computing. Spend by inferring.

A three-step cycle that turns idle hardware into local AI capacity.

01

Run a Mesh Node

Deploy a BlackRoad node on any hardware -- Raspberry Pi, desktop, server, or cloud VM. The node registers with the mesh and begins accepting inference requests routed by the gateway.

02

Earn Credits

Every inference your node completes earns RoadCoin proportional to the compute consumed. Hailo-8 accelerated nodes earn more per request. The 70/30 split allocates 70% to the node operator, 30% to platform operations.

03

Spend on Inference

Use your accumulated credits to run AI inference across the entire mesh. Access any model on any node. The mesh router selects the fastest available node with capacity for your request.
