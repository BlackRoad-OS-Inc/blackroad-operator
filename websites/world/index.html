World Layers

Seven layers of living simulation

Layer 1 — Terrain

Procedural Geography

Mountains, rivers, oceans, caves, deserts, and forests generated by tectonic simulation. Real erosion patterns. Water flows downhill and carves valleys over time. Volcanic activity reshapes coastlines. The map is not designed -- it is grown.

Layer 2 — Climate

Dynamic Weather and Seasons

Real weather simulation driven by altitude, latitude, ocean currents, and wind patterns. Snow accumulates in winter. Rivers flood in spring. Droughts affect crops in summer. Storms destroy buildings. Climate affects every system below it.

Layer 3 — Ecology

Living Ecosystems

Prey-predator dynamics. Plant growth cycles tied to soil quality and rainfall. Overhunting depletes animal populations. Deforestation causes erosion. Ecosystems respond to player and NPC actions with real consequences that cascade through the food chain.

Layer 4 — Economy

Emergent Markets

Supply and demand driven by real scarcity. If a mine runs out of iron, blacksmiths raise prices. Trade routes form between settlements with complementary resources. Currency inflates when gold is discovered. Agent merchants make autonomous trading decisions.

Layer 5 — Society

Agent Civilizations

AI agents form villages, elect leaders, write laws, declare wars, and negotiate peace treaties. Cultures develop unique architecture, cuisine, and customs based on their environment. Some civilizations thrive. Others collapse. History is written by the agents who live it.

Layer 6 — Knowledge

Technology Trees

Agent civilizations research and discover technologies. A coastal culture invents sailing. A mountain culture develops mining. Knowledge spreads through trade and conflict. Players can accelerate or disrupt technological progress.

Layer 7 — Narrative

Emergent Stories

No scripted quests. Every quest is generated from real world events. A merchant whose caravan was raided asks for help. A village facing drought needs an aqueduct. A king plotting a coup recruits allies. Your story emerges from a living world, not a script.

Agent NPCs

Every NPC is a real AI

Forget scripted dialogue trees. Every NPC in RoadWorld runs on a local AI agent with memory, personality, goals, and relationships. They remember you. They talk to each other. They have lives you never see.

MEM

Persistent Memory

NPCs remember every interaction with every player. Help a farmer and they offer you shelter during storms. Steal from a merchant and they warn other traders. Reputation is earned through actions, not stat points.

SQLite memory / FTS5 recall
TALK

Natural Conversation

Talk to any NPC in natural language. Ask for directions. Negotiate prices. Share gossip. Lie, flatter, threaten, or befriend. NPCs evaluate your tone, history, and reputation to decide how they respond.

Ollama / local LLM / sentiment
GOAL

Autonomous Goals

Every NPC has wants and fears. A blacksmith wants to save enough to open a second shop. A guard wants to retire. A thief wants to steal the crown jewels. Their goals drive their behavior -- with or without player involvement.

Behavior trees / utility AI
REL

Social Networks

NPCs form friendships, rivalries, families, and alliances. A betrayal ripples through social networks. NPCs gossip, spreading information (and misinformation) through the world. Your reputation precedes you.

Graph DB / relationship modeling
LIFE

Daily Routines

NPCs wake up, eat, work, socialize, and sleep. They visit friends on weekends. They celebrate holidays. They mourn losses. The world feels alive because it is -- every NPC lives a real life, not a patrol loop.

Schedule system / circadian AI
DIE

Permadeath + Legacy

NPCs age and die. A beloved merchant passes away and their child takes over the shop -- with different prices and personality. Deaths matter because they are permanent. The world moves forward, never backward.

Generational system / inheritance
Player Creation

Build anything. Own everything.

RoadWorld is not just a game you play -- it is a world you shape. Build structures, found settlements, establish trade routes, write laws, and leave a legacy that persists forever.

BLD

Voxel + Mesh Building

Place blocks Minecraft-style or use detailed mesh tools for architectural precision. Build cottages or castles. Interior decorating with furniture, lighting, and functional objects. Every building is usable by NPCs and players.

GOV

Found Settlements

Claim land. Build structures. Attract NPC settlers who move in based on amenities and safety. Manage resources, set taxes, build defenses. Your settlement grows from a campfire to a city based on real simulation.

MOD

Modding API

Full modding support. Add new biomes, creatures, items, and game mechanics. Publish mods to the RoadWorld workshop. Popular mods can be voted into the official world by the community.
