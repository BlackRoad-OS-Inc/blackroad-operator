Questions.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT forgets everything between conversations. Roadie remembers your child's learning history, strengths, weaknesses, and preferred learning style across every session. It also runs locally — your data never goes to OpenAI's servers.

What subjects does it cover?

Math (arithmetic through calculus), science (biology, chemistry, physics), reading comprehension, writing and grammar, history, and geography. We're adding more subjects continuously.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Roadie runs on local AI (Ollama). Once the model is downloaded, it works without internet. Your child can study anywhere.

How does the parent dashboard work?

You see what subjects your child studied, how long they spent, where they struggled, and what they mastered. Weekly email reports summarize progress. No surveillance — just visibility.

Is my child's data safe?

Completely. Roadie runs on your own hardware. No cloud storage, no training on your data, no third-party access. Your family's learning data stays in your home.

What ages is it for?

Elementary through high school. The adaptive difficulty system adjusts to any level — from basic addition to AP Calculus.
