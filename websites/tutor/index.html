The teach-back method

Learn it. Then teach it back.

STEP 01
Assess

Roadie asks diagnostic questions to find exactly where your understanding starts and where it breaks down.

STEP 02
Teach

Concepts are explained at your level with examples that connect to things you already know. Difficulty adapts in real time.

STEP 03
Teach Back

You explain the concept back to Roadie in your own words. The AI evaluates your explanation for accuracy and gaps.

STEP 04
Reinforce

Gaps found during teach-back are addressed immediately. Practice problems lock in understanding. Spaced repetition prevents forgetting.

Live session

A real tutoring conversation.

Roadie
Let's talk about photosynthesis. You mentioned you know plants need sunlight. Can you tell me what happens to that light energy inside the leaf?
You
The chlorophyll absorbs the light and... converts it to chemical energy? Like glucose?
Roadie
Good — you've got the big picture. Chlorophyll absorbs light, and glucose is the end product. But there's an important middle step involving water molecules. Let me walk you through the light reactions...
[...explains light reactions with diagram...]

Roadie
Now teach it back to me. What happens when light hits the chlorophyll, and what role does water play?
You
OK so the light hits chlorophyll and it splits water molecules — that's where the oxygen comes from. The energy from that goes into making ATP, which is used in the Calvin cycle to build glucose.
Verified
Teach-back confirmed: light reactions, photolysis, ATP synthesis, Calvin cycle link. Confidence: 92%. Moving to Calvin cycle detail.
