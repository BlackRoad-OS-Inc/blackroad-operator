Max 12 per circle

Dunbar's number says you can maintain ~12 close relationships. We enforce it. When a circle is full, start a new one.

No algorithm

Posts appear in chronological order. No engagement optimization. No "suggested" content. No dark patterns. Just time.

No ads. Ever.

BackRoad is funded by BlackRoad OS subscriptions. Your attention is not the product. You are not the product. Nobody is the product.

Encrypted by default

Every message, every photo, every voice note — encrypted end-to-end. Not even we can read it. Your circle, your data.

No follower counts

No likes, no shares, no retweets, no follower count. Remove the scoreboard and people start being real.

AI that helps, not hooks

Lucidia helps you write thoughtful replies, remember birthdays, and organize plans. Never suggests you "post more."
