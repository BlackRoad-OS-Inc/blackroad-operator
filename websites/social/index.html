What we don't do.

No Algorithm

Chronological feed. What your friends post, when they post it. No engagement optimization, no rage bait boosting, no "you might like" manipulation.

No Ads

Zero advertising. Not now, not ever. Your attention is not for sale. The platform is funded by BlackRoad OS subscriptions.

No Tracking

No pixel tracking, no fingerprinting, no data brokers. Your social activity stays between you and the people you chose to share it with.

No Engagement Metrics

No like counts, no follower numbers, no viral mechanics. Share because you want to, not because the numbers tell you to.

What we do.

Circles

Share with specific groups of people. Family, close friends, work colleagues, hobby communities. You choose who sees what.

Long-form First

No character limits. Write as much as you need. The platform is designed for depth — essays, reflections, detailed updates — not hot takes.

Creator-Owned

Your content stays yours. Export everything anytime. Delete everything anytime. No platform lock-in, no content hostage.

Sovereign

Runs on BlackRoad infrastructure. Your data lives on Raspberry Pi hardware, not corporate data centers. Self-hosted social networking.
