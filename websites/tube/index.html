For Creators

What YouTube should have been

Built by people who watched the creator economy get strip-mined by platforms that treat content as fuel for ad algorithms.

No Algorithm Suppression

Your subscribers see your videos. Period. No "suggested" content stealing eyeballs. No engagement-optimized feed burying your latest upload. Subscribers get a chronological feed of the creators they follow. The algorithm does not decide who deserves an audience.

  • Feed Chronological. Always. Your subscribers see every upload in order.
  • Reach No throttling based on "engagement score" or content category.
  • Notifications Push notifications work. Every time. For every subscriber.
  • Discovery AI-powered recommendation that promotes quality, not clickbait.

No Demonetization Roulette

Clear, published content guidelines. No mysterious "advertiser-friendly" review. No bot-driven demonetization. No appeal process that takes three weeks. If your content follows the guidelines, it earns revenue. If there is a dispute, a human reviews it within 48 hours.

  • Rules Published, specific, and versioned. No vague "community guidelines."
  • Review Human review within 48 hours for any dispute.
  • Appeals Real appeal process with transparent outcomes and reasoning.
  • Strikes Warnings before strikes. Strikes expire. No permanent marks.
