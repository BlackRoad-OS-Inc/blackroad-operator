NL
Natural Language Editing

Type "trim the silence" or "add a zoom on the speaker at 1:24." RoadView translates your intent into precise timeline edits.

BR
Auto B-Roll

AI identifies talking head segments and suggests relevant b-roll. Accept, swap, or regenerate. Never stare at a static shot again.

VO
AI Voiceover

Generate narration from your script. Multiple voices, adjustable pacing, emotion control. Sounds human because the model was trained on real speech.

CLR
AI Color Grading

Describe the mood: "warm documentary," "cold thriller," "vintage film." The AI applies a grade that matches across all clips.

CAP
Auto Captions

Speech-to-text captions with word-level timing. Styled to match your brand. Animated, positioned, and formatted automatically.

EXP
Multi-Format Export

YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, 4K master. One project, every aspect ratio and format. Batch render overnight.
