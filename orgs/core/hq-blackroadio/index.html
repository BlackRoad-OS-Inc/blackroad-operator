Vision

Your digital space. Your rules.

HQ is a pixel art metaverse that maps to real infrastructure. Each floor represents a function — from the executive rooftop to the basement gym. Agents live here, work here, report here. All rendered on your device.

The building is more than a visual — it is a navigable interface to your infrastructure. Click a floor to see what services run there, which agent is responsible, and what that function does in your network. No cloud rendering. No subscription. Just your HQ.
