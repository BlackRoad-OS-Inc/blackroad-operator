🌐 OPTION 2: Cloudflare (No terminal needed!)

   1. Visit: https://dash.cloudflare.com/
   
   2. Login (or create FREE account)
   
   3. Click: Workers & Pages → Create → Pages
   
   4. Connect to Git:
      - Select: BlackRoad-OS/blackroad-app-store
      - Click: Save and Deploy
   
   5. DONE! Live at: blackroad-store.pages.dev
