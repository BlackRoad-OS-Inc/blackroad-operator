# What IS BlackRoad? - The Ecosystem Explained

**TL;DR**: BlackRoad is a **sovereign AI operating system** with 859 projects building infrastructure for digital independence - local AI, self-hosted services, edge computing, and tools to escape Big Tech dependence.

---

## 🎯 The Big Picture

BlackRoad OS is an **AI-native operating system** designed for:
- **Digital sovereignty** (own your infrastructure)
- **Local-first AI** (run models on your hardware)
- **Multi-agent orchestration** (1000+ AI agents working together)
- **Post-permission computing** (no asking Big Tech for access)

Think: **Linux + Kubernetes + BlackRoad OS, but YOU own it all**
