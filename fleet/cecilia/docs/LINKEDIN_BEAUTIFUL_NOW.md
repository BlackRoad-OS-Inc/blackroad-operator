## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

### Colors
- Primary: #0A0E27 (Dark Blue)
- Accent 1: #FFD700 (Gold)
- Accent 2: #00FFFF (Cyan)
- Text: #FFFFFF (White)

### Fonts
- Headlines: Montserrat Bold or Inter Bold
- Body: Open Sans or Inter Regular

### Image Style
- Dark, professional, tech-forward
- Use geometric patterns (hexagons, circuits, data flows)
- Gold/cyan accents on dark backgrounds
- No stock photos of generic business people
