## Usage Instructions

1. **Schedule tweets** for optimal times:
   - Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday
   - 9 AM or 1 PM (your timezone)
   - Avoid weekends

2. **Engage actively**:
   - Reply to all comments
   - Answer questions promptly
   - Be helpful, not salesy

3. **Track metrics**:
   - Impressions
   - Engagement rate
   - Click-through rate
   - Conversions

4. **Iterate**:
   - Test different hooks
   - A/B test CTAs
   - Double down on what works

---

**Pro tip:** Space out product launches by 3-4 days. Don't spam your audience.
