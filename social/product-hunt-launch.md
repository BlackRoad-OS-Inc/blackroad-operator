## Description
BlackRoad OS is a sovereign edge AI operating system. Five Raspberry Pis, 52 TOPS of local AI inference, 20 domains, 69 AI agents — all for $136/month.

**What it does:**
- Lucidia: AI companion with persistent memory across sessions
- RoadWork: AI tutoring that actually teaches ($9.99/mo)
- Creator Studio: Video, design, and writing tools with 90%+ revenue share
- RoadChain: Blockchain verification and audit trails
- RoundTrip: 69-agent communication hub

**Why it's different:**
- Runs entirely on hardware you own (5 Raspberry Pis)
- Zero cloud dependency — your data never leaves your network
- 52 TOPS of AI inference from 2x Hailo-8 accelerators
- Self-hosted everything: Git, DNS, storage, cache, TLS, VPN
- $136/month total infrastructure vs $2,220-8,400/year cloud

**Who it's for:**
- Developers who want to own their stack
- Educators tired of EdTech that doesn't work
- Creators who want 90%+ of their revenue
- Anyone who believes AI should run on your hardware
